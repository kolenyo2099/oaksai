## Summary
- [[Bay Area Air Quality Management District]] (BAAQMD) confirmed participation in the [[Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility]] (20‑SPPE‑01) Committee Conference on **September 23, 2020**.  
- The conference is part of a small power plant exemption review conducted by the [[California Energy Commission]] (CEC).  
- Email exchanges between BAAQMD staff and CEC hearing officers were documented in docket 234844.  
- The correspondence noted prior BAAQMD interest in backup generator projects, including comments on the Sequoia project at a September 9, 2020 meeting.  

## Key details
- **Project**: Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility (20‑SPPE‑01) – small power plant exemption request.  
- **Docket number**: 234844.  
- **Dates**:
  - Docketed and submitted on **September 21, 2020**.
  - BAAQMD’s earlier conference participation noted on **July 13, 2020**.
  - Sequoia project comment referenced from a meeting on **September 9, 2020**.  
- **Participants**:
  - [[Jakub Zielkiewicz]] – Advanced Projects Advisor, BAAQMD.  
  - [[Ralph Lee]] – Senior Attorney, Office of the Chief Counsel, CEC.  
  - Linda Barrera and Henry Hilken (CCed in emails).  
- **Locations**: San Francisco, CA (BAAQMD office); project site unspecified but within Bay Area jurisdiction.  

## Claims & attribution
- BAAQMD *intended* to participate in the September 23 conference – claim made by Jakub Zielkiewicz.  
- CEC hearing officer Ralph Lee *requested* confirmation of BAAQMD attendance and noted that no substantive comments should be included in the response – claim stated in his email.  
- No independent verification is provided within the text; all statements are sourced directly from the exchanged emails.
