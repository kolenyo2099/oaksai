## Summary
- [[California Energy Commission]] (CEC) adopts a Committee Proposed Decision dated 24 Nov 2021 for the Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility.  
- The facility will comprise **36 × 3.25 MW** and **3 × 500 kW** diesel generators, totaling no more than **100 MW** of capacity.  
- CEC finds that demolition, construction, and operation activities will not cause substantial adverse environmental or energy‑resource impacts.  
- The Small Power Plant Exemption is granted effective 8 Dec 2021; the order was issued by Secretariat Liza Lopez.  

## Key details
- **Project name:** Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility (Backup Generators) and associated Data Center.  
- **Location:** 123, 127, and 131 Great Oaks Boulevard, San Jose, California.  
- **Applicant:** SV1, LLC.  
- **Generators:** 36 × 3.25‑MW reciprocating engines + 3 × 500‑kW standby diesel units.  
- **Capacity limit:** ≤100 MW.  
- **Decision dates:** Committee Proposed Decision – 24 Nov 2021; Errata – 8 Dec 2021.  
- **Order number:** 21‑1208‑2, issued 8 Dec 2021.  
- **Certifying officials:** Hochschild, Gunda; Douglas; McAllister; Monahan (AYE).
