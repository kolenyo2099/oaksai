## Summary
- [[SV1, LLC]] submitted a supplemental response to California Energy Commission data request 47 for the Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility (GOSBGF) application (20‑SPPE‑01).  
- The response details construction emissions estimates for three proposed 21 kV underground feeder trench routes: Via Del Oro, Santa Teresa Route 1, and Santa Teresa Route 2.  
- Emissions are evaluated only for construction; operational emissions are not calculated because the feeders will be underground.  
- Estimated CO₂e emissions per route are 30.3 MT (Del Oro), 50.3 MT (Santa Teresa 1), and 50.2 MT (Santa Teresa 2).  
- Construction timelines span 2022, 2024, and 2027 with trench lengths of 2,920 ft for Del Oro and 5,671 ft each for the Santa Teresa routes.

## Key details
- **Project**: Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility (GOSBGF), a data‑center powered by three new 21 kV feeders.  
- **Routes & Dimensions**  
  - *Via Del Oro*: 2,920 ft length, 4 ft depth, 2 ft width, 2 distribution lines, 1.5 project months (2022).  
  - *Santa Teresa Route 1*: 5,671 ft length, 4 ft depth, 2 ft width, 2 distribution lines, 2.5 project months (2024).  
  - *Santa Teresa Route 2*: 5,671 ft length, 4 ft depth, 2 ft width, 1 distribution line, 2.5 project months (2027).  
- **Equipment & Labor**: Each trench uses 1 excavator, 1 backhoe, 1 conduit truck, 2 other trucks, 8 workers/day, and 8 worker RTs/day; construction lasts 33–55 work days per route.  
- **Emissions Summary (CalEEMod)**  
  - *Del Oro*: NOx 0.00447 t, CO 0.0194 t, SO₂ 0.2523 t, PM₁₀ 0.00034 t, Exh PM₁₀ 0.00055 t, Fug PM₁₀ 0.00116 t, etc.; total CO₂e ≈ 30.3 MT.  
  - *Santa Teresa 1*: NOx 0.00735 t, CO 0.0315 t, SO₂ 0.4198 t; total CO₂e ≈ 50.3 MT.  
  - *Santa Teresa 2*: NOx 0.00727 t, CO 0.0314 t, SO₂ 0.4190 t; total CO₂e ≈ 50.2 MT.  
- **Assumptions**: Balanced cut‑and‑fill (soil reused as backfill), concurrent conduit laying during excavation, average trench progress 180 ft/day based on Southern Regional Water Pipeline Alliance guidelines.

## Claims & attribution
- The emissions figures are claimed by [[SV1, LLC]] and derived from CalEEMod calculations.  
- No operational emissions are reported because the feeders are underground; this claim is stated explicitly in the response.  
- Construction year estimates (2022, 2024, 2027) are presented as "best estimates" based on forecasted need.
