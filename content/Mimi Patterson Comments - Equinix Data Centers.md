## Summary
- [[Mimi Patterson]] submits a public comment on the approval of the Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility, a diesel‑powered small plant operated by [[Equinix Data Centers]].
- She alleges that no arborist or ornithology reports were conducted and that required CEQA public notice signage was omitted.
- Patterson accuses [[Mr. Galati]] (likely an Equinix representative) of downplaying diesel fumes, noise, and environmental impacts such as fuel spills into storm drains.
- She cites violations of the California Environmental Quality Act (CEQA) and requests information on upcoming public forums.

## Key details
- **Project**: Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility – a small power plant providing backup diesel generation for Equinix Data Centers.
- **Docket**: 20‑SPPE‑01, filed September 23 2020 by Mimi Patterson as a public submission.
- **Location**: Neighborhood surrounding the facility; Patterson has lived in the area for 23 years.
- **Regulatory claims**:
  - No arborist or ornithology reports completed.
  - Absence of required 4 × 6 sign/public notice billboard.
  - Alleged CEQA violations concerning environmental and noise impacts.
- **Environmental concerns**: Regular refueling by fuel trucks, diesel spills that reportedly flow into storm drains; noise levels higher than stated.
