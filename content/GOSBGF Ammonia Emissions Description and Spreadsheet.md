## Summary

- The report estimates ammonia (NH3) slip for 36 Tier 4 compliant Cummins engines at **20 ppm** or **0.08 g/bhp‑hr**, yielding about **440 lbs/yr** (≈ 0.61 lb/hr per engine).  
- These emissions are far below BAAQMD Rule 5 thresholds of 7.1 lb/hr and 7,700 lbs/year.  
- NH3 is not a carcinogen but causes acute respiratory and eye irritation; modeled concentrations stay well under the 3,200 µg/m³ REL (acute) and 200 µg/m³ REL (chronic).  
- The analysis includes maintenance‑readiness testing (20 hrs/yr) and emergency operations scenarios (100 hrs/yr), with detailed emission factors for NOx, CO, VOCs, SO₂, PM10/2.5, and CO₂e.  
- All engines are equipped with aftercoolers and diesel particulate filters; the facility operates on ULSD fuel.

## Key details

- **Facility**: Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility (TN # 237205).  
- **Engine make/model**: Cummins QSK95‑G9, 36 units.  
- **Fuel**: ULSD (7.05 lb/gal; 139 000 Btu/gal).  
- **Warm‑up period**: 15 min at D2 cycle (assumed zero NH3 slip).  
- **Steady‑state period**: 45 min at Tier 4 values (20 ppm NH3).  
- **Composite emission factor**: 0.06 g/bhp‑hr for NH3.  
- **Annual NH3 emissions**: 440 lbs, 0.61 lb/hr per engine.  
- **Regulatory comparison**: BAAQMD thresholds – 7.1 lb/hr, 7,700 lbs/yr (facility well below).  
- **Health effects**: Acute REL 3,200 µg/m³; chronic REL 200 µg/m³; modeled impact < 3–5 µg/m³ for NOx and ~ 200 µg/m³ for NH3.  
- **Scenario 1 (M&R + Emergency)**: Total annual NH3 ≈ 9,359 lb (0.61 lb/hr per engine during M&R; negligible during emergency).  
- **Scenario 2 (BAAQMD evaluation)**: Similar totals with slightly higher NOx due to D2 cycle usage.
