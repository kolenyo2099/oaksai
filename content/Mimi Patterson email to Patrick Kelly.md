## Summary
- [[Mimi Patterson]] emails [[Patrick Kelly]], a Supervising Planner in San Jose, questioning the approval process for Equinix's data center and small power plant project at Great Oaks South.  
- She accuses the City of San Jose Planning and Development Department of bypassing public outreach, violating zoning requirements, and permitting an expanded generator count without proper review.  
- The email chain includes replies from planners including [[Angela Wang]], [[Robert Manford]], [[Rosalynn Hughey]], and others, who defend the process as compliant with existing procedures.  
- Patterson references a 2016–2017 “bare bones staff” situation and cites a recorded Zoom call where staff acknowledged errors in the original review.  

## Key details
- Project: Equinix Great Oaks Data Centers – three large data centers totaling 579,000 sq ft on an 18.56‑acre site with a small power plant.  
- Original permit: SP15‑031 (Special Use Permit) approved in 2016–2017; amended to include 36 generators instead of the original 21.  
- Key personnel cited: [[Patrick Kelly]] (Planner IV, Development Review), [[Angela Wang]] (Planner III), [[Robert Manford]] (Deputy Director), [[Rosalynn Hughey]] (Director).  
- Patterson’s concerns focus on lack of community outreach, failure to post required signs in 2015/2016, and alleged violations of the City of San Jose Planning Guide #105.
