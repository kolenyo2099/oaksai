## Summary  
- The [[California Energy Commission (CEC)]] Committee requested supplemental information on the application for a small power plant exemption (SPPE) for the [[Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility]].  
- Questions focus on environmental impacts of the proposed [[Santa Teresa Substation]] and new 21‑kV distribution feeders, noise thresholds, cumulative impact analysis, NOx offsets, and greenhouse gas mitigation measures.  
- Responses must be filed by 5:00 p.m. on September 2, 2021.  

## Key details  
- Docket No.: **20-SPPE-01** (TN 239482).  
- Project: [[Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility]] – a small backup generating plant in California.  
- FEIR submitted July 28, 2021; addendum August 18, 2021.  
- Substation: [[Santa Teresa Substation]] approved by the [[California Public Utilities Commission (CPUC)]], with PG&E as operator.  
- Noise threshold: 10 dBA construction‑related noise, questioned for consistency with City of San Jose’s General Plan Policy EC‑1.7.  
- NOx offsets: to be confirmed via permitting with the BAAQMD; comparison between Tier 4 and Tier 2 equipment emissions modeling requested.  
- Greenhouse gas mitigation: revised MM GHG‑1 proposed as alternative to San Jose Clean Energy Total Green program; compliance with California Code of Regulations §§15064.5, 15183.5 examined.
