## Summary  
- [[California Energy Commission]] (CEC) scheduled a pre‑hearing conference on **September 7, 2021** and an evidentiary hearing on **September 13, 2021**, both to be held remotely.  
- The proceedings concern a Small Power Plant Exemption (SPPE) application for the [[Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility]] and its associated [[Great Oaks South Data Center]] in [[San Jose]], California.  
- The applicant, [[SV1, LLC]]—a wholly owned subsidiary of [[Equinix, LLC]]—seeks to build 36 diesel‑fired generators (3.25 MW each) capable of supplying up to **99 MW** to the data center.  
- A draft Environmental Impact Report filed on **May 21, 2021** concluded that the project would not produce significant adverse environmental impacts and that mitigation measures were adequate.  
- The CEC will evaluate the application against three criteria: capacity ≤ 100 MW, no substantial adverse environmental impact, and no substantial adverse impact on energy resources.  

## Key details  
- **Project site:** 123‑131 Great Oaks Boulevard, San Jose; ~18 acres undeveloped.  
- **Data Center:** Three two‑story buildings totaling ~547,050 sq ft for secure server housing.  
- **Backup Generators:** 36 units (3.25 MW each) arranged in six on‑site yards; 12 per building, with two redundant units per building.  
- **Life‑safety generators:** Three 0.5‑MW units (one per building) for fire suppression and emergency systems.  
- **Power supply:** Generators will operate only to feed the data center and are not connected to the electric distribution grid; they may run for testing, maintenance, or power loss events from [[Pacific Gas and Electric Company]] (PG&E).  
- **DEIR findings (May 21 2021):** No significant impacts on air quality, biological resources, cultural resources, geology/soil, noise, agriculture, forestry, minerals, wildfire, energy resources, hazards, hydrology, land use, population, recreation, transportation, or utilities.  

## Claims & attribution  
- **Claim:** The DEIR determined that the project would not produce any significant environmental impacts. **Attribution:** California Energy Commission staff (Staff).  
- **Claim:** The CEC will grant an SPPE if it finds capacity ≤ 100 MW and no substantial adverse impacts on environment or energy resources. **Attribution:** CEC statutory authority under the Public Resources Code and CEQA guidelines.  
- **Unconfirmed:** Whether any intervenors have joined the proceeding; the deadline to petition was **July 6, 2021**.
