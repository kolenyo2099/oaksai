## Summary
- [[Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility]] is the subject of a committee conference.
- The conference, scheduled for Wednesday, September 23 2020, was delayed by 30 minutes and will begin at 1:30 p.m. after opening Zoom at 1:00 p.m.
- The notice was sent by [[Ralph Lee]], Co‑hearing Officer for the Great Oaks proceeding, to various Energy staff and external stakeholders.
- Email recipients included members of the California Energy Commission, ARB (Air Resources Board) staff, and other energy industry contacts.
- The email explicitly requests no responses and indicates that all correspondence will be filed in the docket.
