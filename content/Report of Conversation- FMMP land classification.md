## Summary
- Conversation between [[Jeanine Hinde]] (CEC Planner II) and [[Kerri Kisko]] (CDOC Environmental Scientist) about the Farmland Mapping and Monitoring Program (FMMP) classification of APNs 706‑02‑057 and 706‑02‑060 in San Jose.  
- FMMP classifies the site as **Farmland of Local Importance** based on 2016 data, unchanged through 2018 unless fallow for three update cycles.  
- Under CEQA Appendix G, Farmland of Local Importance is not counted as “Farmland”, so the project does not trigger a significant impact review.  
- The site lies along Via Del Oro between San Ignacio Ave and Great Oaks Blvd in [[San Jose]].  
- 2018 NAIP imagery shows the area fallow; a change to Grazing Land would occur only if it remains fallow through 2022.

## Key details
- **Project**: Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility (Small Power Plant Exemption).  
- **Location**: APNs 706‑02‑057 & 706‑02‑060, Via Del Oro, San Jose.  
- **FMMP status (2016)**: Farmland of Local Importance.  
- **FMMP status (2018)**: Expected to remain Farmland of Local Importance unless fallow for three cycles → Grazing Land in 2022.  
- **Imagery**: 2018 NAIP, 2019 Santa Clara County‑SCCMap, Google Streetview April 2019 all show fallow land.  
- **CEQA relevance**: Farmland of Local Importance not counted as “Farmland”; therefore no significant impact under Appendix G.  
- **Communication dates**:  
  - 1 Apr 2020 – Hinde emails Kisko for classification info.  
  - 2 Apr 2020 – Kisko replies with FMMP details and CEQA implications.

CEQA implications. CEQA Appendix G determines an area to be potentially significant if: a) Convert Prime Farmland, Unique Farmland, or Farmland of Statewide Importance (Farmland), as shown on the maps prepared pursuant to the Farmland Mapping and Monitoring Program of the California Resources Agency, to nonagricultural use? (see the Agriculture and Forestry Resources section at: https://resources.ca.gov/CNRALegacyFiles/ceqa/docs/ab52/final‐ approved‐appendix‐G.pdf) Notice that Farmland of Local Importance is not listed. Under CEQA only the three categories that include irrigated farmland count as “Farmland”. So, this project is not considered Farmland under CEQA and therefore is not considered a significant impact.
