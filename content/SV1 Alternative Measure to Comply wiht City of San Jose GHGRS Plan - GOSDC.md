## Summary  
- [[Equinix]] proposes the Equinix Clean Energy Program as an alternative measure to meet the City of San Jose 2030 GHG Reduction Strategy for the Great Oaks South Data Center (GOSDC).  
- The program claims 100 % renewable energy coverage in the U.S. since 2018, supported by wind farm PPAs, local utility green tariffs, and Green‑e certified REC purchases.  
- Equinix argues that enrolling GOSDC in this program obviates the need for MM GHG‑1 mitigation measures outlined in TN239063.  
- The City of San Jose would require annual verification documentation from Equinix to confirm continued compliance.  
- The proposal references the California Energy Commission and CEQA Section 15183.5 to classify the strategy as a qualified Climate Action Plan with less-than-significant impact.  

## Key details  
- **Project:** Great Oaks South Data Center (GOSDC) – a data center operated by Equinix under the SV1, LLC applicant.  
- **Alternative Measure:** Equinix Clean Energy Program, described in Attachment 1 of the submission.  
- **Renewable Sources:**  
  - Two wind farm PPAs (225 MW total) that became operational in 2016.  
  - Local utility green tariffs.  
  - Unbundled Green‑e certified REC purchases with vintage <5 years.  
- **Compliance Claims:**  
  - Equinix’s program meets or exceeds the GHG Reduction Strategy’s “Renewable Energy Development” requirement.  
  - The alternative measure renders MM GHG‑1 unnecessary for compliance.  
- **Verification Requirement:** Annual submission of documentation similar to Attachment 2 (2020 verification) to City of San Jose.  
- **Regulatory Context:** CEQA Section 15183.5 – qualified Climate Action Plan; impact classified as less than significant.  

## Claims & attribution  
- **Equinix claims** that its Clean Energy Program achieves the same or greater GHG reductions compared to MM GHG‑1, citing 225 MW of new renewable generation secured through PPAs and a commitment to <5‑year vintage REC purchases.  
- **City of San Jose’s position** (as implied by the submission) is that acceptance of the alternative measure would satisfy the 2030 strategy without additional mitigation.  
- **Unconfirmed/Not independently verified:** The exact quantitative GHG reduction figures and independent audit results are not provided in the text; verification relies on Equinix’s internal reporting.
