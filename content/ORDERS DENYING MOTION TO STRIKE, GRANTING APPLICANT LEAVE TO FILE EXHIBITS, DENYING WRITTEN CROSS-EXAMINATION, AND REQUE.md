## Summary

- [[State Energy Resources Conservation and Development Commission]] (CEC) denied a motion to strike that would have removed testimony from [[Robert Sarvey]].
- The motion was filed by the applicant, a wholly owned subsidiary of [[Equinix]], LLC, in a small power plant exemption proceeding for the Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility.
- CEC granted the applicant leave to file additional exhibits and allowed written cross‑examination, citing fairness, hearing efficiency, and adequacy of record.
- The Committee held a prehearing conference on September 7 2021 and scheduled an evidentiary hearing for September 21 2021.
- Staff argued that Sarvey’s reply testimony was untimely; Sarvey countered that it was directed to earlier opening testimony.

## Key details

- **Docket number:** 20‑SPPE‑01 (Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility)
- **Project parties:** Applicant – wholly owned subsidiary of [[Equinix]]; Staff – CEC staff.
- **Key dates:**
  - March 19 2020 – application filed
  - May 13 2020 – Committee designated
  - August 18 2021 – Staff filed addendum to Final EIR
  - September 3 2021 – Staff and Committee responded to motion
  - September 7 2021 – prehearing conference
  - September 21 2021 – evidentiary hearing scheduled
- **Environmental findings:** Draft and Final Environmental Impact Reports concluded no significant impacts with mitigation.
- **Motion to strike grounds:** Unfairness, gamesmanship, procedural violation of June 24th scheduling order.
- **Committee decision criteria:** Fairness to parties, hearing efficiency, adequacy of record.
