## Summary  
- [[Ralph Lee]] (Senior Attorney, California Energy Commission) invited [[California Air Resources Board]] (CARB) to comment at the September 23, 2020 Committee Conference on the Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility.  
- [[Craig Segall]], Assistant Chief Counsel, CARB confirmed CARB will attend the conference and kept enforcement staff informed.  
- The project is a small power plant exemption (SPPE) application, docketed 20‑SPPE‑01, for a backup generator in the Bay Area.  
- Email correspondence was exchanged on September 17, 2020 between the hearing officer and CARB staff.  

## Key details  
- **Docket number:** 20‑SPPE‑01 (Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility)  
- **Event date:** September 23, 2020 – Committee Conference (CEC Committee)  
- **Participants:**  
  - [[Ralph Lee]] (California Energy Commission)  
  - [[Craig Segall]] (CARB Assistant Chief Counsel)  
  - Enforcement Division staff: [[Wesley Dyer]], [[Linda Barrera]]  
  - Other CARB staff: [[Todd Sax]], [[Courtney Graham]], [[David Mehl]]  
- **Locations:** Sacramento, CA – 1001 I Street (CARB headquarters)  
- **Project context:** backup generators in the Bay Area; interest from CARB to contribute to CEC review.  

## Claims & attribution  
- **Claimed by CARB (Craig Segall):** CARB will plan to appear at the conference; staff were notified and remain on standby for logistical changes.  
- **Claimed by CEC (Ralph Lee):** CARB has expressed interest in providing public comment and is invited to attend the September 23 conference.  
- **Uncertainty:** No independent verification of attendance beyond the email confirmation; no substantive comments were provided in the correspondence.
