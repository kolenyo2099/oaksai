## Summary
- [[Great Oaks South Data Center]] was the subject of a public scoping meeting on **November 17 2020**.
- The meeting was organized by the [[California Energy Commission]] (CEC) Public Advisor’s Office.
- Participants could engage informally via Zoom or formally as intervenors, presenting evidence and filing motions.
- Key participation avenues included the Staff's Environmental Assessment, pre‑hearing conferences, evidentiary hearings, committee decisions, and a final CEC business meeting decision.
- Contact details: phone (916) 654‑4489, email PublicAdvisor@enegy.ca.gov, address 1516 Ninth Street, MS‑10, Sacramento, CA.

## Key details
- **Docket**: 20‑SPPE‑01 (TN # 235684).
- **Project title**: Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility Small Power Plant Exemption.
- **Meeting date**: November 17 2020; presentation recorded on November 18 2020.
- **Filer**: Rosemary Avalos, Public Advisor, California Energy Commission.

  - *Formal*: Intervenor status—present evidence, cross‑examine witnesses, file motions; petition to intervene without attorney requirement.
- **Deadlines**: Filing and discovery deadlines set by the Presiding Member (details not specified).
- **Information access**: https://ww2.energy.ca.gov/sitingcases/greatoakssouth/ for documents and subscription list serve.
