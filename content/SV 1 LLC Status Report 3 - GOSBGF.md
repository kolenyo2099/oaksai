## Summary
- [[SV1]] files Status Report Number 2, updating project details for the [[Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility (GOSBGF)]].
- The report focuses on revising the project description and assessing noise, air quality, and public health impacts of installing **Tier 4 compliant engines**.
- SV1 expects to submit revised analysis by March 10, 2021 and has already docketed a draft VMT analysis (TN 236541) dated February 1, 2021.
- The report urges staff to finalize unaffected sections of the Draft Environmental Impact Report (EIR).

## Key details
- **Project**: Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility (GOSBGF), a small power plant exemption under TN #236721.  
- **Entity filing**: [[SV1]] (operated by DayZenLLC).  
- **Location of filer**: 1720 Park Place Drive, Carmichael, CA 95608.  
- **Engine specification**: Transition to Tier 4 compliant engines for the backup generating facility.  
- **Timeline**: Draft VMT analysis docketed February 1, 2021; revised project description and related analyses due by March 10, 2021.  
- **Regulatory context**: Follows Committee Orders TN 234539 dated August 31, 2020 issued by the State of California Energy Resources Conservation and Development Commission.

## Claims & attribution
- SV1 claims it has submitted all outstanding items except for the revised project description and Tier 4 engine analysis, which are pending.  
- The report asserts that staff should complete EIR sections unaffected by the engine change.  
- These claims originate from the status report filed by [[Scott A. Galati]], counsel to SV1.
