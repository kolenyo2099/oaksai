## Summary  
- The California Energy Commission held a remote Committee Conference on **September 23, 2020** via Zoom to discuss the Small Power Plant Exemption (SPPE) application for [[Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility]] by [[SV1, LLC]].  
- Staff proposed preparing an Environmental Impact Report (EIR) for the project; parties were invited to comment on this and on public outreach opportunities.  
- The conference included representation from state agencies such as the California Air Resources Board and Bay Area Air Quality Management District, plus local government input from the City of San Jose and Council Member Sergio Jiménez.  
- Committee procedures emphasized remote participation rules under Governor Newsom’s Executive Orders N‑25‑20 and N‑29‑20 to address COVID‑19 precautions.  

## Key details  
- **Date & time:** 1:30 p.m., Wednesday, September 23, 2020 (remote via Zoom).  
- **Participants:**  
  - Presiding Member: [[Karen Douglas]] (Commissioner)  
  - Associate Member: [[David Hochschild]] (Chair)  
  - Hearing Officers: [[Ralph Lee]] and [[Linda Barrera]]  
  - Advisors: [[Kourtney Vaccaro]], [[Eli Harland]], [[Le‑Quyen Nguyen]], [[Jon Hilliard]]  
  - Staff: [[Lisa DeCarlo]] (Counsel), [[Lisa Worrall]] (Project Manager)  
  - Applicant: [[Scott Galati]] representing [[SV1, LLC]] (Equinix subsidiary).  
- **Government agencies present:**  
  - California Air Resources Board – Wesley Dyer  
  - Bay Area Air Quality Management District – Jakub Zielkiewicz  
  - City of San Jose – Lucas Ramirez (Council Member Sergio Jiménez).  
- **Agenda items:**  
  1. Staff’s proposal to prepare an EIR.  
  2. Opportunities for expanded public outreach and engagement.  
  3. Feasibility of holding an in‑person event in San Jose, California.  
  4. Status of discovery, outstanding issues, schedule, and next steps.  
- **Public comment period:** Approximately 64 minutes allocated; closed session followed by adjournment.
