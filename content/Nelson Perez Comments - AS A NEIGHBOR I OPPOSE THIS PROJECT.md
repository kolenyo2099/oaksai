## Summary  
- [[Nelson Perez]] opposes the Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility.  
- Concerns include removal of large Oak trees and visual impact on nearby hills.  
- Claims that the plant’s 36 generators will produce noise, pollution, and a constant “hum” even when idle.  
- Asserts proximity to residential areas makes the project unacceptable.  

## Key details  
- Docket Number: 20‑SPPE‑01 (TN #: 233742).  
- Project: Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility – Small Power Plant Exemption.  
- Site location: within one mile of Perez’s residence, near residential area.  
- Planned equipment: 36 generators (type unspecified).  
- Environmental concerns cited: tree removal, noise, pollution, visual obstruction.  

## Claims & attribution  
- [[Nelson Perez]] states that the project will “block the views we have from the hills” and be “visually unpleasant.”  
- He claims the plant’s generators will generate noise and pollution when turned on and a constant “hum” even when idle.  
- No independent verification of these impacts is provided in the text; statements are unconfirmed.
