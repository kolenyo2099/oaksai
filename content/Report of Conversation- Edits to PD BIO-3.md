## Summary

- [[California Energy Commission]] staff requested changes to nesting bird survey timing in mitigation measure PD BIO‑3 for the [[Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility]] project.  
- The applicant, represented by [[Scott A. Galati]] of [[DayZen LLC]], agreed to the edits and confirmed acceptance via email.  
- Proposed new survey windows: no more than 14 days before construction during February 1–April 30 and August 31; 30 days for May–August.  
- Survey scope limited to a 250‑foot buffer around active nests, with 100‑foot buffer for non‑raptor species.  
- Final agreement documented in a conversation report dated June 15 2020.

## Key details

- **Project**: Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility (Small Power Plant Exemption) – docket 20‑SPPE‑01.  
- **Mitigation measure**: PD BIO‑3 – bird nesting survey timing and buffer zones.  
- **Timeline changes**:
  - Feb 1–Apr 30 & Aug 31: surveys ≤14 days before construction.  
  - May–Aug: surveys ≤30 days before construction.  
- **Buffer distances**: 250 ft for raptors, 100 ft for other birds; maintained until post‑breeding season or fledging confirmed by ornithologist.  
- **Stakeholders**:
  - Staff: Lisa Worrall, Senior Environmental Planner, CEC.  
  - Applicant rep: Scott A. Galati, President, DayZen LLC.  
- **Documentation**: Email exchanges (May 28 and June 5, 2020) and a formal conversation report (June 15, 2020).

This is from our biology staff: The applicant proposes project design measure PD BIO-3 to reduce impacts to nesting birds. However, the measure places different timing of when to conduct these surveys along with different timeframes (months) during the breeding season. Some birds can complete a nest within 14 days, therefore 30 days is too long a time frame and would allow a bird to build a nest and lay eggs which would prohibit tree removal where the nest occurs and would reduce construction work in this area because a buffer would be needed to protect the bird and the nest. So my changes include conducting surveys no more than 14 days prior to construction for the entire breeding season (February 1 through August 31). Staff proposes the following changes in bold for new text and strikethrough for deleted text to reduce impacts to less than significant for nesting birds. PD BIO-3: The following measure will be implemented to reduce impacts to nesting birds: · If possible, construction should be scheduled between September and January (inclusive) to avoid the nesting season. If this is not possible, pre-construction surveys for nesting raptors and other migratory breeding birds shall be conducted by a qualified ornithologist to identify active nests that may be disturbed during project implementation onsite and within 250 feet of the site. Between February 1 and April (inclusive) August 31 pre-construction surveys shall be conducted no more than 14 days prior to the initiation of construction activities or tree relocation or removal. Between May and August (inclusive), pre-construction surveys shall be conducted no more than thirty (30) days prior to the initiation of these activities. The surveying ornithologist shall inspect all trees in and immediately adjacent to the construction area for nests. · If an active nest is found in or close enough to the construction area to be disturbed by these activities, the ornithologist shall, in consultation with the California Department of Fish and Wildlife (CDFW), designate a construction- free buffer zone (typically 250 feet for raptors and 100 feet for other birds) around the nest, which shall be maintained until after the breeding season has ended and/or a qualified ornithologist has determined that the young birds have fledged. · The applicant shall submit a report indicating the results of the survey and any designated buffer zones to the satisfaction of the Director of Planning, Building and Code Enforcement prior to the issuance of any grading or building permit. Our staff biologist believes these changes are reasonable and consistent with staff's mitigation for the other data centers and with other IS/MND projects in the area. Scott, please let me know if you are in agreement or not with these changes to survey timing. Thanks, Lisa Worrall
