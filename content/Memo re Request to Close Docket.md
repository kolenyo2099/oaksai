## Summary
- [[California Energy Commission]] (CEC) requests closure of docket **20-SPPE-01** concerning the Great Oaks South Generating Facility.  
- CEC granted a Small Power Plant Exemption on December 8, 2021 for the facility.  
- The memo includes a final Proof of Service list with contacts from various stakeholders.  
- Hearing Officer [[Ralph Lee]] forwarded the request to the Docket Unit on May 20, 2022.  

## Key details
- **Docket Number**: 20-SPPE-01  
- **Project Title**: Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility Small Power Plant Exemption  
- **Exemption granted**: December 8, 2021 (TN 240883)  
- **Request date**: May 20, 2022, 2:17 PM  
- **Proof of Service List contacts**: includes representatives from [[Equinox]], [[DayZen LLC]], and several CEC staff members.  

## Claims & attribution
- Claim: The docket should be closed. Source: Memo authored by [[Ralph Lee]] on behalf of the California Energy Commission.  
- Uncertainty: No independent verification provided in the memo; closure status depends on administrative action by the Docket Unit.
