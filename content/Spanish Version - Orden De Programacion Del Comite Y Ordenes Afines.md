## Summary
- [[SV1, LLC]] (subsidiary of [[Equinix, LLC]]) submitted a Small Power Plant Exemption (SPPE) request for the Great Oaks South backup generator facility on 19 March 2020.  
- The California Energy Commission (CEC) convened a committee that held conferences on 13 July and 23 September 2020 to discuss whether an Environmental Impact Report (EIR) is required.  
- The CEC staff recommended preparing an EIR, citing concerns from the California Air Resources Board (CARB) and Bay Area Air Quality Management District (BAAQMD) about diesel generator emissions.  
- The applicant agreed that the law permits an EIR but argued it would be unnecessary and delay the project; the committee ultimately endorsed the staff’s recommendation for an EIR.  
- The committee also addressed public outreach, suggesting community conferences where feasible and permitting the applicant to post signage if practicable.

## Key details
- **Project**: Great Oaks South backup generator facility (small power plant).  
- **Applicant**: [[SV1, LLC]] (100 % owned by [[Equinix, LLC]]).  
- **Docket numbers**: 20‑SPPE‑01 (SPPE), TN 232466–232467‑3 (request), TN 233721 (July 2 notice), TN 233795 (July 7 staff memo), TN 233832 (July 10 applicant response), TN 234657 (August 7 staff memo), TN 234660 (September 11 applicant memo).  
- **Committee**: chaired by [[Karen Douglas]] (Commissioner, Chair) and [[David Hochschild]] (President, Associate Member).  
- **Key dates**:
  - 19 Mar 2020 – SPPE filing.  
  - 2 Jul 2020 – Committee notice; conference scheduled 13 Jul.  
  - 7 Jul 2020 – Staff memo on issues and program.  
  - 10 Jul 2020 – Applicant response.  
  - 7 Aug 2020 – Updated staff memo recommending EIR.  
  - 31 Aug 2020 – Notice for conference 23 Sep; request for updated status reports by 10 Sep.  
  - 23 Sep 2020 – Conference discussing EIR and public participation.  
- **Environmental assessment**: Staff argued an EIR is required to address diesel emissions, model emergency scenarios, and satisfy CEQA’s purpose of informing the public. Applicant contended that an IS/MND would suffice.  
- **Public outreach**: Committee encouraged community conferences when possible; allowed applicant to post signage at the site if feasible.
