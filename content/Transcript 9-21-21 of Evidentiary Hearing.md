## Summary

- The hearing was held remotely via Zoom on **September 21 2021** to discuss the application for a **Small Power Plant Exemption (SPPE)** for the *Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility*.
- The applicant, **SV1, LLC**, a wholly owned subsidiary of **Equinix**, sought exemption from the Energy Commission’s exclusive jurisdiction over thermal power plants with capacities of 5 MW or more.
- The project includes a data center and backup generating facility located at **123‑127 Great Oaks Boulevard, San Jose, California**.
- Committee members were **Karen Douglas** (Presiding Member) and **David Hochschild** (Chair of the Energy Commission). Hearing officer was **Ralph Lee**.
- Public comment and potential closed‑session deliberations were scheduled after testimony.

## Key details

- **Project scope**: three two‑story buildings totaling ~547,000 sq ft for a data center; backup generating facility to provide power if PG&E fails.
- **Applicant**: SV1, LLC (Equinix subsidiary).
- **Location**: 123‑127 Great Oaks Boulevard, San Jose, CA.
- **Hearing logistics**: Conducted remotely via Zoom; participants muted/unmuted using phone controls *6 and *9; court reporter present.
- **Regulatory context**: SPPE allows exemption from licensing requirements for plants ≥5 MW under the Energy Commission’s jurisdiction.
