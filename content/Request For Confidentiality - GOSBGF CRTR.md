## Summary
- [[SV1, LLC]] requests confidential designation for its Cultural Resources Technical Report on the [[Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility (GOSBGF)]].
- The report was prepared by [[Albion Environmental Inc.]] in October 2018 and covers APNs 706‑02‑053 to 706‑02‑056 in San Jose, California.
- Confidentiality is sought indefinitely to prevent looting of potential cultural resources identified in the report.
- SV1 argues that disclosure would reveal trade secrets and sensitive site locations protected under Government Code §§ 6254(e), (k) and (r).
- The application is submitted to the [[California Energy Commission]] by counsel Scott Galati on behalf of SV1.

## Key details
- **Project**: Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility (GOSBGF)
- **Applicant**: [[SV1, LLC]], a wholly owned subsidiary of [[Equinix, LLC]]

- **Locations**: San Jose, California; office addresses: 1720 Park Place Drive, Carmichael, CA 95608; One Lagoon Drive, Redwood City, CA 94065
