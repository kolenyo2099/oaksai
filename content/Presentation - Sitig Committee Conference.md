## Summary
- [[Equinix]] presented the Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility (GOSBGF) at a Siting Committee Conference on 7 July 2020.  
- The project proposes three new two‑story buildings (SV12, SV18, SV19) covering 547,050 GSF with an electrical demand of up to 99 MW.  
- Each building will house 12 × 3.25 MW generators plus a 0.5 MW life‑safety unit, totaling 36 generators across the complex.  
- The GOSBGF is intended primarily for maintenance, testing and emergency outages, with limited routine use due to PG&E grid reliability.  
- A Special Use Permit (SUP) and Mitigated Negative Declaration (MND) were approved by the City of San Jose on 1 February 2017, covering backup generating facilities.

## Key details
- Site: 18 acres in an urban area bounded by Via Del Oro (north), Great Oaks Boulevard (east), vacant land (south), and San Ignacio Avenue (west).  
- Total building footprint: 547,050 GSF; projected peak demand: 99 MW.  
- Generators: 36 × 3.25 MW (12 per building) + 3 × 0.5 MW life‑safety units.  
- Generator placement: exterior yards of SV12, SV18, and SV19 designated as “Generator or Equipment yard”.  
- Electrical distribution includes MV Switchgear, LV Switchgear, UPS, ASTS, and cabling interconnecting the six generation yards to their respective buildings.  
- Water usage reduced by switching from water‑cooled to air‑cooled chillers, achieving 3.36 acre‑ft/yr domestic consumption.

## Claims & attribution
- [[Equinix]] claims that the GOSBGF will rarely be used except for maintenance and emergencies due to high PG&E reliability.  
- The company asserts that the backup generators provide a safety net to maintain power during outages, ensuring continuous server operation.  
- These statements are presented in the conference materials; independent verification is not provided within the text.
