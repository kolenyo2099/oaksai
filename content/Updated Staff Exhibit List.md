## Summary

- [[California Energy Commission]] staff filed an updated exhibit list on September 8, 2021 for the [[Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility]] small power plant exemption application (TN 239637).
- The update adds a new document to the record and assigns a TN number to exhibit 205.
- Exhibits include the Final Environmental Impact Report (TN 239063), staff opening testimony (TN 239258), an addendum to the EIR (TN 239361), City of San Jose acceptance letter (TN 239476), CEC responses to committee questions and intervenor testimony (TNs 239582 & 239587), and additional staff declarations (TN 239592).
- The filing follows a Notice of Prehearing Conference, Evidentiary Hearing, and Scheduling Order issued on June 24, 2021.

## Key details

- **Project title:** Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility
- **Docket number:** 20‑SPPE‑01 (TN 239637)

- **Documents added:**
  - Exhibit 205 – CEC Staff Response to Intervenor Sarvey’s Reply Testimony (TN 239587)
  - TN number assigned to exhibit 205
- **Subject areas covered by exhibits:** Environmental impact, air quality, GHG, noise, transportation, biological resources, cultural resources, geology, hazardous materials, hydrology, water quality, mandatory findings of significance, alternatives.
- **Locations referenced:** Sacramento (CECom office), San Jose (City acceptance letter).
