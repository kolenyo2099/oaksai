## Summary
- [[Claire A Warshaw]] thanks the California Energy Commission (CEC) staff for preparing a detailed 524‑page Draft Environmental Impact Report (EIR) on the Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility.  
- Highlights include extensive health‑risk analysis, emergency generator usage data, alternative fuel discussions, noise control measures, and biodiversity mitigation ($300/tree).  
- Warshaw raises questions about ADA ramp provisions, cumulative air quality assessment radius, diesel tank size, and proximity to an international airport.  
- Suggestions call for updated diesel particulate data, social media outreach, photovoltaic integration on the 18‑acre site, grey‑water design, and EMF analysis.  
- Concerns are voiced over potential loss of CEC jurisdiction if emergency diesel generators dominate during large‑scale events.

## Key details
- Project: Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility (small power plant exemption, docket 20‑SPPE‑01).  
- Location: Santa Clara County, near San Jose; 18 acres with a 6‑mile cumulative air quality assessment radius.  
- Diesel storage: 8,500–9,600 gallons in above‑ground tanks; emergency generator capacity 1,000 horsepower meeting U.S. EPA Tier 4 standards (per Bay Area Air Quality Management District guidelines).  
- Health and environmental data cited from pages 103‑106 (pollution types), 464‑469 (2020 emergency use), 373, 431‑433, 437, 443 (nitrogen deposition fees).  
- Noise: ambient residential rule of 55 dB (p. 314).  
- Renewable diesel vs biodiesel explanation on pages 432‑436, 496.  
- Distance to nearest residence: 650–710 feet; closest international airport lies northwest (p. 126).

## Claims & attribution
- **Claimed by Warshaw**: The EIR provides valuable education for data center projects and includes comprehensive health risk and mitigation information.  
- **Attribution**: Statements are attributed to Warshaw’s review of the CEC‑prepared draft; no independent verification is provided in the text.  
- **Unconfirmed / Not independently verified**: Questions about ADA ramp legality, cumulative air quality radius validity, diesel tank size accuracy, and exact airport direction remain unverified.
