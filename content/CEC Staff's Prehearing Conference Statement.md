## Summary  
- [[California Energy Commission]] issued a prehearing conference statement on the Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility Small Power Plant Exemption application.  
- All subject areas are deemed complete and ready for evidentiary hearing; no technical delays reported.  
- Staff will submit all initial direct testimony in writing, with exhibits numbered 200–205 covering environmental impact reports, addenda, and staff responses to committee questions.  
- No disputes exist between staff and the applicant; any dispute is limited to intervenor [[Robert Sarvey]].  
- Staff does not intend to sponsor live witnesses or question other parties’ witnesses at the evidentiary hearing.

- **Docket**: 20‑SPPE‑01, TN 239563.  
- **Project**: Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility (Small Power Plant Exemption).  
- **Date of scheduling order**: June 24 2021; statement filed August 31 2021.  
- **Exhibits**:

  - 203 – City of San Jose Acceptance of MMRP and Mitigation Compliance (TN 239476).  
  - 204 – CEC Staff Response to Order Requesting Supplemental Information (TBD).  
  - 205 – CEC Staff Response to Intervenor Sarvey’s Reply Testimony (TBD).  
- **Witnesses**: None proposed for live testimony; all staff testimony is written.
