## Summary

- [[Energy Commission of California]] (CEC) re‑scheduled the Probatory Hearing for the Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility from 13 September 2021 to **21 September 2021 at 10:00 a.m.**  
- The hearing will be held remotely via Zoom in compliance with Executive Order N‑08‑21, continuing pandemic‑related remote participation.  
- The request for a Small Power Plant Exemption (SPPE) was submitted by [[SV1, LLC]], a wholly owned subsidiary of [[Equinix, LLC]] on 19 March 2020 to exempt the facility from CEC jurisdiction over thermal plants ≥ 50 MW.  
- The re‑scheduling decision was made during a Pre‑Hearing Conference held 7 September 2021 where committee members and parties confirmed availability for the new date.  
- All documents, including the hearing notice and prior orders, are available online at the CEC e‑filing portal.

## Key details

- **Project**: Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility – a small power plant exemption request (SPPE) under docket 20‑SPPE‑01.  
- **Original hearing date**: 13 September 2021; **New date**: 21 September 2021, 10:00 a.m. (remote).  
- **Committee members**: 
  - [[Karen Douglas]] – Commissioner and Chair of the CEC.  
  - [[David O. Hochschild]] – President of the CEC and committee member.  
  - Robert Sarvey – Intervenor.  
- **Submission date for exemption request**: 19 March 2020 (TN 232466).  
- **Pre‑Hearing Conference**: 7 September 2021; confirmed verbal agreement from all parties to attend the rescheduled hearing.  
- **Remote participation**: Zoom link – https://energy.zoom.us/j/91494360781?pwd=UW9kdWNIaW9xUS94QURXQW4yNVh6Zz09 (ID 914‑9436‑0781, password GOSPPE@10).  
- **Public participation**: Call-ins via toll‑free numbers (877‑853‑5257, 888‑475‑4499, 669‑219‑2599) and telephone “raise hand” codes (*9 for hand, *6 to mute).  
- **Documents**: Available on the CEC e‑filing portal (https://efiling.energy.ca.gov/Lists/DocketLog.aspx?docketnumber=20-SPPE-01) and the project website (https://www.energy.ca.gov/powerplant/reciprocating-engine/great-oaks-south-generating-facility).
