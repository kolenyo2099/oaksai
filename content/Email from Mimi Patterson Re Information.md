## Summary
- [[Mimi Patterson]] sent an email to [[Ralph Lee]] and the California Energy Commission (CEC) on 12 Oct 2020, requesting documents related to the Great Oaks South SPPE proceeding.  
- The CEC staff responded that the email was sent in error and provided a link to a notice of a recent Great Oaks South SPPE event.  
- Mimi’s original request asked for: a copy of the Declaration of Posting of Equinix signs, conceptual elevations of the project, and the name of the PM who worked on the project 2015‑17.  
- Ralph Lee noted he would file the correspondence in the Great Oaks South SPPE docket.  

## Key details
- **Project**: Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility – a small power plant exemption (SPPE) application in California.  
- **Date of email exchange**: 11–12 Oct 2020, with timestamps 3:27 PM and 8:03 AM respectively.  
- **Link provided by CEC**: https://efiling.energy.ca.gov/GetDocument.aspx?tn=234539&DocumentContentId=67369 (notice of a recent Great Oaks South SPPE event).  
- **Requested documents**:  
  - Declaration of Posting of Equinix signs (2015) with pictures.  
  - Conceptual elevations for the project, similar to those in the China Mobile data center entitlement package.  
  - Name of the PM who worked on the project between 2015‑17.  

## Claims & attribution
- **Claim**: Mimi Patterson believes she has access to or is entitled to the documents listed above.  
  - *Attribution*: Ms. Patterson’s email (subject “Fwd: Information”).
