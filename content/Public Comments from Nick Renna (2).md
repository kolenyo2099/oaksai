## Summary
- [[Nick Renna]] submits public comments opposing the **Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility**, citing disruption, noise, pollution, and explosive risks.  
- Concerns that construction would lower property values during its seven‑year build phase.  
- Residents claim they were not notified of the project in December 2016, a fact revealed at a councilman/planning commission meeting on 8/14/2020.  
- The planning commission admitted the notification lapse but confirmed the project, approved in January 2017, will proceed.  
- Renna indicates potential legal action and urges neighbors to voice opposition.

## Key details
- **Project**: Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility – small power plant exemption TN #234331.  
- **Approval**: Project authorized by planning commission in January 2017.  
- **Construction timeline**: Seven‑year build period.
