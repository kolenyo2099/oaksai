## Summary
- [[SV1, LLC]] files a prehearing conference statement for its Small Power Plant Exemption (SPPE) application for the [[Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility]] (GOSBGF).  
- SV1 asserts that all subject areas are complete and ready for adjudication at the evidentiary hearing.  
- The company proposes to provide testimony in writing, reserving oral testimony only if a motion to strike [[Mr. Sarvey]]’s testimony fails.  
- Witnesses proposed include [[Masoud Zafaripour]], [[David Smith]], [[Bruce Frandsen]], and [[Michael Lisenbee]].  
- No cross‑examination is requested at this stage; SV1 may cross‑examine Mr. Sarvey pending the motion outcome.

## Key details
- **Application**: 20-SPPE-01, filed by DayZenLLC on 31 Aug 2021 for a small power plant exemption under the Energy Resources Conservation and Development Commission.  
- **Document list**: 33 exhibits ranging from the main SPPE application (232466) to supplemental responses and environmental analyses, totaling over 3,000 pages.  
- **Witnesses**: Four named individuals, each having filed opening or reply testimony.  
- **Testing format**: All evidence will be presented in writing unless oral testimony is required for disputed topics.  
- **Timeline**: The statement was submitted on 31 Aug 2021; evidentiary hearings are pending per the Committee Order dated 19 Jun 2021 (TN 238471).
