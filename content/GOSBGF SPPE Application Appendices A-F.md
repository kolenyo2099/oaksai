## Summary
- [[Cummins]] engines (36 units of model QSK95-G9 and 3 units of model QSX15-G9) are proposed for the Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility.
- Emissions estimates are provided under various load scenarios, with NOx, CO, SO2, PM10/PM2.5 figures in g/bhp‑hr.
- The facility is applying for a Small Power Plant Exemption (SPPE) under docket 20-SPPE-01, submitted by [[DayZenLLC]] on 3 19 2020.
- No Diesel Particulate Filters are installed; emissions calculations use Tier 2 and 40 CFR 89 D2 cycle‑weighted factors.
- The application includes detailed stack parameters (height, diameter, velocity) and fuel characteristics for ULSD.

## Key details
- **Project**: Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility (GOSBGF)
- **Docket Number**: 20-SPPE-01
- **Application Date**: 3 19 2020 2:29 PM
- **Filers**:
  - [[DayZenLLC]] – Applicant Representative
  - Scott Galati – Submittor
- **Engine Details**:
  - 36 units of model QSK95‑G9 (Cummins) – max 6 tested per day, not concurrent.
  - 3 units of model QSX15‑G9 (Cummins) – max 3 tested per day, not concurrent.
- **Fuel**: ULSD (Ultra‑Low Sulfur Diesel)
- **Stack Specs (QSK95‑G9)**:
  - Height: 1800 ft; Diameter: 222 in; Exit Area: 2.181662 sq.ft
- **Stack Specs (QSX15‑G9)**:
  - Height: 1800 ft; Diameter: 34 in; Exit Area: 0.785398 sq.ft
- **Emissions Factors**:
  - Tier 2 for CO when > Nominal, 40 CFR 89 D2 for NOx/PM.
  - No Diesel Particulate Filters installed.

## Claims & attribution
- The application claims that the proposed generators meet SPPE criteria and provides emission estimates based on ISO 8178‑1 data and Cummins memos dated 9 17 19.  
- These figures are presented as *declared emergency operations* and *maintenance/readiness testing* scenarios; they are not independently verified in the document.
