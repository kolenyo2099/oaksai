## Summary
- [[California Energy Commission]] staff reports all outstanding data for the Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility project has been received.  
- The applicant submitted revised noise, transportation, air‑quality, public health, and ammonia emission analyses between March 12 2020 and March 18 2021 (TNs 237148, 231749, 237150, 237152, 237205).  
- Staff confirms the project will use Tier 4‑compliant diesel engines for the Initial Study/Draft Environmental Impact Report (EIR).  
- The draft EIR is on track to be published per the Committee’s scheduling order.  

## Key details
- **Project**: Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility Small Power Plant Exemption (Docket 20-SPPE‑01, TN #237405) located in California.  
- **Timeline**:  
  - September 23 2020 – Committee orders monthly status reports starting September 10 2020.  
  - March 12 2020 – submission of revised noise, project description, transportation, air‑quality and public health analyses (TNs 237148, 231749, 237150, 237152).  
  - March 18 2021 – submission of ammonia emissions spreadsheet (TN 237205).  
- **Engine specification**: Tier 4‑compliant diesel engines to be used in the Initial Study/Draft EIR.
