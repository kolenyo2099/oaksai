## Summary
- The City of [[San Jose]] prepared an addendum to the Final Environmental Impact Report (FEIR) for the [[Great Oaks Mixed Use Project]] and the Envision San Jose 2040 General Plan FEIR to accommodate minor changes.
- Changes include rezoning to allow data centers, increasing allowable square footage from 260 000 sq ft to 400 000 sq ft, reducing parking requirements, and removing setback restrictions for Area 2.
- The addendum covers a proposed Equinix Data Center comprising two two‑story buildings totaling ~386 000 sq ft on an ~11.15 acre site near Highway 85.
- Environmental impacts reviewed (traffic, cultural resources, energy, air quality, etc.) were found not to create new significant effects; thus no new EIR is required.
- The addendum will be attached to the FEIR but not publicly circulated per CEQA § 15164(c).

## Key details
- **Project title:** Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility Small Power Plant Exemption (TN #239577)
- **Location:** Southwest side of Great Oaks Boulevard, ~1 000 ft northwest of Highway 85; APN 706‑09‑117 and –118.
- **Lead agency:** City of [[San Jose]] Planning, Building and Code Enforcement
- **Property owner/applicant:** Equinix (data center operator)
- **Zoning change:** From A(PD) Planned Development to allow data centers; square footage increase from 260 000 to 400 000 sq ft.
- **Data center footprint:** Two buildings, ~386 000 sq ft total, on 11.15 acres.
- **Regulatory approvals:** File Nos. PDC15‑059 and PD15‑031; CEQA addendum under § 15164.
- **Environmental assessments:** Reviewed impacts in traffic, cultural resources, urban services, aesthetics, energy, water quality, soils, hazardous materials, biotic resources, airport considerations, greenhouse gas emissions, utilities, noise, land use, air quality, microclimate, construction period.
- **Mitigation measures:** Sound walls and attenuation for backup generators; monitoring and reporting program attached.
