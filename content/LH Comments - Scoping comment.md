## Summary

- LH raises environmental and safety questions for the **Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility** project.
- Concerns include water conservation, climate impact, green building standards, diesel leak risk, blast area hazards, proximity to **Santa Teresa Light Rail Station**, use of Tier 4 equipment, and transit‑friendly design features.
- LH specifically urges procurement of 100 % clean energy from **San Jose Clean Energy**.
- The comment references coordination with the **Santa Clara Valley Transportation Authority (VTA)** and contact David J. Powers and Associates for environmental staff.

## Key details

- Project: Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility, a small power plant exemption (TN # 235804) under docket 20‑SPPE‑01.
- Filing party: LH (public submission).
- Date of comment: 11/24/2020 23:13:38 PM; docketed on 11/25/2020.
- Location: near **Santa Teresa Light Rail Station**, an end‑of‑line facility with storage yard, light rail platform, bus transit center, operator facility, and park‑and‑ride lot.
- Specific inquiries:
  - Water conservation measures for data centers (indoor/outdoor, recycled water).
  - Climate change impact; compliance with state greenhouse gas goals.
  - LEED or other green building standards (recycling materials, natural lighting).
  - Diesel tank leak detection systems and groundwater contamination risk.
  - Blast area of generators and public health risks to nearby Kaiser facilities, day‑care centers, residents, schools.
  - Use of Tier 4 construction equipment for air quality.
  - Features promoting alternative commutes (bike/pedestrian access, EV charging).

## Claims & attribution

- LH claims that the project **must** employ water conservation measures and potentially recycled water; this is an assertion pending verification.
- LH states the project should be compliant with state greenhouse gas reduction goals; unverified in the text.
- LH encourages purchasing 100 % clean energy from **San Jose Clean Energy**; this is a recommendation, not a fact.
- LH questions whether Tier 4 equipment will be used during construction; claim awaiting confirmation.
- LH asserts that blast area risks exist and must be discussed; no independent verification provided.

DOCKETED Docket Number: 20-SPPE-01 Project Title: Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility Small Power Plant Exemption TN #: 235804 Document Title: LH Comments - Scoping comment Description: N/A Filer: System Organization: LH Submitter Role: Public Submission Date: 11/24/2020 11:13:38 PM Docketed Date: 11/25/2020 Comment Received From: LH Submitted On: 11/24/2020 Docket Number: 20-SPPE-01 Scoping comment 1) What water conservation measures will the data centers employ? In what ways can water conservation be improved for both indoor and outdoor uses (i.e. irrigation and landscaping)? Will recycled water be used? 2) What impact will the project have on climate change? Is the project in compliance with State goals to reduce greenhouse gas emissions? I strongly encourage the project applicant to purchase 100 percent clean energy from San Jose Clean Energy. 3) Will the data centers be designed to achieve LEED or other green building standards by using recycling materials, natural lighting, and other measures to reduce energy, water, and other natural resources? 4) What would happen if the leak detection systems for the diesel tanks failed? What is the risk to groundwater contamination and how is this risk being minimized or avoided? 5) What is the "blast area" of the generators? Please thoroughly discuss the public health risks associated with the project particularly the risks to Kaiser facilities, day care centers, residents, and schools. 6) The project is located near Santa Teresa Light Rail Station, an end-of-the-line facility which encompasses a storage yard, light rail platform, bus transit center, operator facility, and park-and-ride lot. Please coordinate with the Santa Clara Valley Transportation Authority (VTA) regarding electrical substations and other operations that may be impacted by the project. David J. Powers and Associates has the contact information for environmental staff at VTA. 7) Will Tier 4 equipment be used during construction of the project to minimize air quality impacts? 8) The project should include features (e.g. improved access to bike and ped facilities, EV charging) that promote alternative commutes to reduce employee VMT.
