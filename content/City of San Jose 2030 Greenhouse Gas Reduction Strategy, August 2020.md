## Summary
- [[City of San José]] released its 2030 Greenhouse Gas Reduction Strategy in August 2020.  
- The strategy outlines emissions targets, reduction pathways, and implementation frameworks for buildings, land use, transportation, recycling, waste, and other sectors.  
- It aligns with California’s Climate Change Scoping Plan, the Clean Air Act, and regional programs such as BAAQMD.  
- Key personnel include Project Manager [[Meenaxi Raval]] (AICP) and lead author [[Joshua Lathan]].  
- The document lists contributions from various city departments: Environmental Services, Planning, Building & Code Enforcement, Clean Energy, Transportation, and sustainability partners like ICLEI.

## Key details
- **Target year:** 2030.  
- **Baseline year:** 2017 (inventory data).  
- **Sector focus:** Buildings, energy, land use/transportation, recycling/waste, other GHG reduction areas.  
- **Regulatory context:** Federal Clean Air Act, EPA mandatory GHG reporting, CAFE and SAFE vehicle rules; state-level climate targets and executive orders; regional BAAQMD GHGRS elements.  
- **Implementation steps:** Development review integration, monitoring progress, evaluation, and evolution of strategies.  
- **Contributors:** Environmental Services Department (Ken Davies), Planning & Building & Code Enforcement (Robert Manford), Clean Energy Department (Kevin Meehan), Transportation Department (Wilson Tam), ICLEI partners (Agustin Leon Cuello), city planning staff (Josh Pollack, Tanya Shreyner).

## Claims & attribution
- The strategy claims to reduce overall GHG emissions by a specified percentage relative to the 2017 baseline; exact numeric target is detailed in Table 3.5 but not quoted here.  
- Attribution: The document is produced by the City of San José’s Planning, Building and Code Enforcement Department under Project Manager [[Meenaxi Raval]] and authored by [[Joshua Lathan]].  
- Uncertainty: Specific quantitative targets and projected reduction potentials are presented in tables within the full PDF; these figures are not reproduced here.
