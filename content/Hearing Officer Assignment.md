## Summary
- The California Energy Commission assigned [[Rebecca Westmore]] as Hearing Officer for the Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility SPPE (Docket No. 20‑SPPE‑01).  
- The assignment was made by Chair [[David Hochschild]] on May 20, 2020.  
- The hearing officer will assist the committee in conducting proceedings on the application for certification of the facility.  
- The memorandum was addressed to the presiding member and associate members, including [[Karen Douglas]].
