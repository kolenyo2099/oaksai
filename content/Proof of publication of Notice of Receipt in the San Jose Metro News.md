## Summary  
- [[California Energy Commission]] filed a proof of publication for the notice of receipt regarding the Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility (GOSBGF).  
- The notice was published in the daily newspaper [[San Jose Mercury News]] on 9 July 2020.  
- The filing confirms that the GOSBGF, a small power plant exemption project, is located in San Jose and will provide up to 50 MW of auxiliary power.  
- The document references the Small Power Plant Exemption (SPPE) process and the CEC’s role as lead agency for environmental review under CEQA.  
- Contact information for the CEC’s public affairs office and project coordinator is provided in the filing.

## Key details  
- **Project**: Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility (GOSBGF), also referred to as GOSDC, a 50 MW auxiliary power plant exemption project under docket 20‑SPPE‑01.  
- **Location**: City of San Jose, County of Santa Clara, State of California.  
- **Publication Date**: 9 July 2020 in the regular issue of [[San Jose Mercury News]] (no supplement).  
- **Filer**: Lisa Worrall, Commission Staff at the California Energy Commission.  
- **Document type**: Proof of publication of Notice of Receipt, docket number 20‑SPPE‑01.  
- **Regulatory context**: SPPE allows applicants to propose up to 50 MW of auxiliary power for small facilities; CEC leads CEQA review and permits.  
- **Contact**: Senior Environmental Project Manager Uisa Worrall (email: uisa.worral@cec.ca.gov, phone (916) 654‑4515).
