## Summary
- [[Mimi Patterson]] emailed [[Angela Wang]] questioning the handling of permits for the Great Oaks South data center project.
- She highlighted concerns about lack of public notice, inadequate signage, and rapid approval by Director [[Harry Freitas]] after a brief review.
- The email references the 2017 Special Use Permit SP15‑031 and its amendment SPA15‑031‑01, noting changes in generator count and site footprint.
- Patterson asks for clarification on entitlement procedures, tree removal decisions, and the city’s allowance of amendments instead of new applications.
- City officials replied that the project was approved under a Special Use Permit, with limited public outreach due to the 500‑foot notice radius, and that subsequent amendments are treated as standard development proposals.

## Key details
- Project: Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility – three data center buildings totaling 579,000 sq ft on an 18.56‑acre site.
- Original permit: SP15‑031 (Special Use Permit) approved at a Director’s Hearing on 1/25/2017; notice mailed 1/6/2017 with a 500‑foot radius.
- Amendment: SPA15‑031‑01 (Special Use Permit Amendment) to add 15 generators (total 36), reduce footprint by ~32,000 sq ft.
- Director of Planning at time of original approval: [[Harry Freitas]]; review period reportedly three weeks with no community outreach.
- Tree removal: 13 heritage oak trees (9 ordinance‑size, 4 non‑ordinance) removed per city decision.
- Signage: Current on‑site sign 2′×3′; Patterson argues it should be 4′×6′ for projects ≥100,000 sq ft.
- CEQA involvement: California Energy Commission to determine Small Powerplant Exemption and potentially prepare an Environmental Impact Report (EIR).

## Claims & attribution
- **Claimed by Patterson**: No public notice was sent in 2017; lack of minutes and hearing audio; rapid approval without community outreach; inadequate signage; tree removal lacked justification.  
  *Unconfirmed* – city officials denied missing notices, citing archived audio recordings.
- **City’s response**: Approval followed standard procedures for a Special Use Permit; amendment treated as Standard Development Proposal; public hearing not yet scheduled; signage requirements pending final classification.  
  *Attribution*: City of San Jose Planning and Development Department.
