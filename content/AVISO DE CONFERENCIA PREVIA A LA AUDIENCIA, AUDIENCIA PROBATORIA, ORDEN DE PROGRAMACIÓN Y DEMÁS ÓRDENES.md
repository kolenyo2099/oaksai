## Summary
- [[California Energy Commission]] (CEC) scheduled a **pre‑conference** for 7 Sept 2021 and an **adjudicatory hearing** for 13 Sept 2021, both remote.  
- The hearing concerns a **Small Power Plant Exemption (SPPE)** request for the **Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility** at 123‑131 Great Oaks Boulevard, San Jose, California.  
- The project involves a data center and 36 diesel‑powered generators totalling up to 99 MW; it is exempt from CEC jurisdiction if it meets SPPE criteria.  
- A DEIR (Draft Environmental Impact Report) was issued on 21 May 2021 finding no significant impacts, pending mitigation measures.  
- Parties to the process include the applicant [[SV1, LLC]] (subsidiary of [[Equinix, LLC]]) and CEC staff; intervention is open until 6 July 2021.

## Key details
- **Project location**: 123‑127‑131 Great Oaks Boulevard, San Jose, CA – ~18 acres, undeveloped.  
- **Data center**: three two‑story buildings, ≈547,050 ft², housing secure servers.  
- **Backup generators**: 36 units, 3.25 MW each, arranged in six on‑site yards; total capacity 99 MW. Each building hosts 12 units plus two redundant ones; one 0.5 MW safety generator per building for life‑support systems.  
- **Schedule**:
  - Pre‑conference: 7 Sept 2021, 10:00 a.m., remote.
  - Adjudicatory hearing: 13 Sept 2021, 10:00 a.m., remote.  
- **Legal framework**: California Public Resources Code §§ 25120, 25500; CEQA requirements for “all action” (data center + generators).  
- **Exemption scope**: If the plant generates between 50–100 MW and meets environmental criteria, CEC may exempt it from its jurisdiction over San Jose City and Bay Area Air Quality Management District.  

## Claims & attribution
