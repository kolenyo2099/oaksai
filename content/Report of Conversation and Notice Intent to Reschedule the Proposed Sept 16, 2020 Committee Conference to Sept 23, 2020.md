## Summary  
- The [[California Energy Commission]] (CEC) moved the Committee Conference for the Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility from **16 Sept 2020** to **23 Sept 2020 at 1 p.m.**  
- The change was requested by attorney [[Scott Galati]] on behalf of applicant [[SV1, LLC]] (subsidiary of [[Equinix, LLC]]) due to a scheduling conflict.  
- Parties may object or file a conflict notice no later than noon **31 Aug 2020**.  
- No formal conference notice had been issued prior to the rescheduling decision.  

## Key details  
- **Project:** Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility (Small Power Plant Exemption, TN # 234519).  
- **Original proposed date:** 16 Sept 2020 at 1:00 p.m.  
- **New conference date:** 23 Sept 2020 at 1:00 p.m.

No formal notice of a September 16, 2020 Committee Conference has been issued, but the parties were asked to hold that date by the August 26, 2020 Hearing Officer Memorandum.2 During the August 26, 2020 call, Mr. Galati requested rescheduling the Committee Conference to the week of September 21st. Mr. Galati stated that if rescheduling is not possible, Mr. Galati would make arrangements to attend the September 16, 2020 Committee Conference. Mr. Lee responded that he would see if there was anything the Committee can do. The Committee now intends to reschedule the formerly proposed September 16, 2020 Committee Conference to the new date and time of September 23, 2020 at 1 p.m. Parties may file any objection to rescheduling the Committee Conference to the new date and time of September 23, 2020 at 1 p.m., or may file notice of conflict, no later than noon on Monday, August 31, 2020. 1 On March 19, 2020, SV1, LLC, a wholly owned subsidiary of Equinix, LLC, (Applicant) submitted an application for a Small Power Plant Exemption for the Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility.
