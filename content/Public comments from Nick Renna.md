## Summary  
- [[Nick Renna]] submits public comments regarding the proposed [[Great Oaks Backup Generating Facility]].  
- Concerns raised include air quality, prolonged construction noise (seven years), storage of 331 000 gallons of diesel fuel, and potential negative impact on home values.  
- Claims that no neighbors were notified in 2017 when the project was approved; this was allegedly confirmed by [[Patrick Kelly]] of the planning committee during a meeting with [[San Jose]] City representatives.  
- Renna urges action to halt the project, citing possible grounds for litigation.

## Key details  
- Project: Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility (Small Power Plant Exemption TN #234329).  
- Comment filed by Lisa Worrall on behalf of Nick Renna; submission date 8/17/2020.  
- Meeting referenced occurred on 8/14/2020 with San Jose City representatives and planning committee members.  
- Specific metrics: 331,000 gallons diesel storage; seven years of construction noise.  

## Claims & attribution  
- **Claim**: No neighbor was notified in 2017 about the project approval.  
  - **Attribution**: Nick Renna (public comment).  
- **Claim**: The lack of notification is a “glaring failure” by the planning commission and may constitute grounds for litigation.  
  - **Attribution**: Nick Renna.  
- **Verification**: Patrick Kelly, planning committee member, allegedly confirmed the claim during the meeting.
