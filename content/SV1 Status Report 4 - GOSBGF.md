## Summary
- [[SV1]] filed Status Report Number 4 on March 10, 2021 under docket 20‑SPPE‑01 for the Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility.  
- The report states that SV1 will revise its project description and air quality analysis to reflect a switch from Tier 2 to Tier 4 compliant engines by March 12, 2021.  
- A traffic analysis draft is being finalized after receiving comments from the City and is expected to be docketed by the same deadline.  
- SV1 anticipates submitting all outstanding items by March 12, 2021 and urges the staff to publish its Draft Environmental Impact Report in April 2021.
