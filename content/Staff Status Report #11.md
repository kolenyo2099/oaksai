## Summary
- [[California Energy Commission]] (CEC) issued **Status Report #11** for the Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility Small Power Plant Exemption (20‑SPPE‑01).  
- CEC staff granted a request from the City of San Jose to extend the comment period until July 13, 2021.  
- The extended period was intended to allow the city to review the Draft Environmental Impact Report (EIR) before submitting comments.  
- Commenting closed on July 6, 2021 for all other stakeholders; CEC received three comment letters, including one from the applicant.  
- CEC is reviewing these comments and expects further input from the City of San Jose.
