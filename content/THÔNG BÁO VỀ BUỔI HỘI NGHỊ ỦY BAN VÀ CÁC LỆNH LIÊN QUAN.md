## Summary  
- [[California Energy Commission]] (CEC) scheduled a committee meeting on **23 September 2020** to discuss the Small Power Plant Exemption (SPPE) request for the [[Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility]].  
- The SPPE application was filed by [[SV1, LLC]], a wholly owned subsidiary of [[Equinix, LLC]] on 19 March 2020.  
- The proposed project includes a three‑building data center (~547 000 ft²) and a backup power plant with **36 diesel generators** (3.25 MW each, totaling up to 99 MW).  
- CEC staff intend to prepare an Environmental Impact Report (EIR) for the project, with a “Notice of Preparation” expected in August 2020.  
- Public participation will be via Zoom; attendees must use the provided ID and password.

## Key details  
- **Meeting date & time:** 23 Sep 2020 at 1:00 p.m. (remote).  
- **Location:** Sacramento, CA (CEC headquarters, 1516 Ninth Street).  
- **Project site:** 123–131 [[Great Oaks]] streets in [[San Jose]], California; ~18 acres undeveloped.  
- **Data center:** Three two‑story buildings, 547 050 ft² total, with climate‑controlled server rooms.  
- **Backup plant:** 36 diesel generators (3.25 MW each), 12 per building; 2 designated as backup for redundancy; plus three 0.5 MW safety generators (one per building).  
- **Capacity:** Up to 99 MW, meeting the maximum load of the data center.  
- **Regulatory context:** CEQA requires CEC to treat the entire project as a single “work” for environmental review.  
- **Timeline updates:** Staff submitted an August‑7 memo updating the July‑7 memo, proposing an initial schedule and potential mitigation statement (MND).  
- **Reporting requirements:** Parties must submit monthly status reports no later than the 10th of each month starting 10 Sep 2020.

## Claims & attribution
