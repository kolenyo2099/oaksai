## Summary

- [[DayZenLLC]] filed emission data for a new backup power plant at [[USA Equinix San Jose]].
- The facility will use a single [[Cummins QSK95-G9]] diesel engine, rated 3250 kW, running 200 hours per year.
- Engine emissions are reported under EPA method 320 (FTIR) with NOx at 5.7 g/bhp‑hr and CO at 0.5 g/bhp‑hr.
- Emission reductions compared to target values show 91.2 % NOx reduction, 87 % CO reduction, and 73 % PM10 reduction.
- The plant will use Ultra‑Low Sulfur Diesel (ULSD) with ≤1 wt % sulfated ash.

## Key details

- **Project name**: Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility Small Power Plant Exemption TN #237508
- **Location**: [[USA Equinix San Jose]]
- **Engine**: 1 × [[Cummins QSK95-G9]], 1800 RPM, 3250 kW
- **Fuel**: Ultra‑Low Sulfur Diesel (ULSD), ≤1 wt % sulfated ash or less
- **Operating hours**: 200 h/yr
- **Emission data** (100 % load):
  - NOx: 5.7 g/bhp‑hr (521 tons/yr)
  - CO: 0.5 g/bhp‑hr (75 tons/yr)
  - NMHC: 0.06 g/bhp‑hr
  - PM10: 0.11 g/bhp‑hr
- **Reduction targets**:
  - NOx reduction 91.2 %
  - CO reduction 87 %
  - PM10 reduction 73 %
- **Regulatory reference**: EPA method 320 (FTIR) for NMHC composition.
