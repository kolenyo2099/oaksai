## Summary  
- [[Lisa Cordray]] opposes the Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility small power plant.  
- Claims the site is too close to residential areas and hills.  
- Expresses concern that smoke/exhaust would linger during low‑wind periods, increasing health risks.  
- Suggests relocating closer to 101 and higher up on the hills if no alternative exists.

## Key details  
- **Project**: Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility Small Power Plant Exemption (Docket Number: 20-SPPE-01).  
- **Submission date**: July 3, 2020 at 6:49 PM.  
- **Filer**: Lisa Cordray; **Submitter Role**: Public Submission.  
- **Location concerns**: proximity to residential zones on Santa Teresa and Great Oaks; hilltop positioning near highway 101.  
- **Health issue cited**: potential lingering smoke/exhaust during periods of low wind or no wind.

## Claims & attribution  
- Claim: The plant’s location is too close to residential areas and hills, leading to possible health problems from lingering exhaust.  
  - Attribution: [[Lisa Cordray]] (public submission).  
- Suggestion: Move the plant closer to 101 and higher on the hills if no other alternative exists.  
  - Attribution: Same source; unverified by external evidence in the text.
