## Summary

- [[Jeffrey R. Shelton]] and [[Lluvia Shelton]] oppose the proposed Great Oaks South backup power plant.
- They argue the site is on a residential street, raising concerns about emissions, noise, and testing activities affecting nearby homes.
- The comments request reconsideration to avoid placing a Small Power Plant in this area.

## Key details

- Project: Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility (Small Power Plant).
- Docket Number: 20‑SPPE‑01; TN #: 234050.
- Submission date: 7/28/2020 7:26:46 PM.
- Filer: System Organization: Jeffrey R. Shelton.
- Location: residential street within the Great Oaks South area.
- Concerns highlighted: emissions, noise, test operations, impact on families living nearby.

## Claims & attribution

- Claim: The backup generation project should not proceed as planned because it will be built on a residential street and will expose residents to emissions and noise.  
  Attribution: [[Jeffrey R. Shelton]] (public submission).
- Claim: Residences would share the street with the Small Power Plant, potentially disrupting family life (sleeping, eating, playing).  
  Attribution: [[Jeffrey R. Shelton]].
- Unconfirmed: Specific emission levels or noise thresholds are not provided in the text.

Shelton Submitted On: 7/28/2020 Docket Number: 20-SPPE-01 Great Oaks South is in Residential Area To whom it may concern, The Great Oaks South backup generation project should not proceed as planned. There are neighborhoods with families that live, play, sleep and eat Please reconsider this area and do not place a Small Power Plant on this site. Thanks, Jeff and Lluvia Shelton
