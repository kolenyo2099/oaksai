## Summary  
- [[Ngoc Tran]] of [[Equinix, LLC]] requested a small power plant exemption for the [[Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility]].  
- The California Energy Commission (CEC) scheduled a Prehearing Conference on September 7, 2021 and an Evidentiary Hearing on September 13, 2021.  
- Intervenor [[Robert Sarvey]] filed reply testimony on August 24, 2021; the applicant then filed a Motion in Limine to strike that testimony on August 27, 2021.  
- The CEC granted the applicant’s request for an order shortening time, setting September 8, 2021 as the deadline for responses and disallowing further briefing or oral argument.

In addition to the Motion to Strike, Applicant seeks an order shortening time, specifically requesting that the Committee hear and consider the Motion to Strike at the Prehearing Conference and requests a shortened time for the filing of responses to the Motion to Strike.8 DISCUSSION AND ORDER In general, responses to motions are due within 14 days after the motion is filed.9 The Presiding Member has authority to regulate the conduct of the proceeding, including the discretion to shorten the time allowed to respond to motions in a manner that preserves the parties’ notice and provides an opportunity to be heard.10 Here, the fourteenth day after the date the Motion to Strike was filed is September 10, 2021. While the Committee will not consider the Motion to Strike at the Prehearing Conference, it will issue an order shortening time to resolve the Motion to Strike prior to the Evidentiary Hearing to help conduct an orderly proceeding. The Committee therefore GRANTS the request for an order shortening time to respond to the Motion to Strike and ORDERS as follows: the parties shall file any response to the Motion to Strike no later than 5:00 p.m. on September 8, 2021.
