## Summary  
- The **California Energy Commission** (CEC) issued key dates for the *Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility* SPPE proceeding.  
- Deadline to file a petition to intervene is **July 6, 2021**, 45 days after staff published the draft EIR on May 21, 2021.  
- A prehearing conference is tentatively scheduled for **September 7, 2021** at 10:00 a.m., and an evidentiary hearing for **September 13, 2021** at 10:00 a.m.  
- Local, state, federal agencies, tribal governments, and the public may attend or submit written comments; no need to be an intervenor to participate.  
- Contact details for participation assistance are provided via the CEC Public Advisor’s Office.

## Key details  
- **Docket number:** 20‑SPPE‑01 (Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility).  
- **Committee Scheduling Order** issued Oct 16, 2020; all deadlines therein remain in effect.  
- **Draft EIR publication date:** May 21, 2021 → triggers the 45‑day petition window ending July 6, 2021.  
- **Prehearing conference:** Sept 7, 2021, 10:00 a.m. (tentative).  
- **Evidentiary hearing:** Sept 13, 2021, 10:00 a.m. (tentative).  
- **Public comment submission:** email to docket@energy.ca.gov or e‑comment portal; include docket number and proceeding name in subject line.  
- **Interpreting services** (Spanish, Vietnamese, etc.) must be requested ≥5 days before the hearing at 5:00 p.m. by phone or email.
