## Summary  
- The applicant confirms that [[DPF]]s will be installed on all 39 engines (36 large QSK95‑G9 and 3 small QSX15‑G9).  
- Emissions data for PM10 are based on a conservative factor of 0.015 g/bhp‑hr, but the vendor claims compliance with the 0.01 g/bhp‑hr standard.  
- Construction equipment listed by [[Atmospheric Dynamics, Inc.]] (ADI) was unchanged between May and August 2020 CalEEMod submissions.  
- Operational HRA modeling has been corrected to use urban dispersion coefficients instead of rural ones.  
- Several typographical errors in tables (e.g., cancer risk values, VOC monthly averages, NOx emissions for overlapping period) have been identified and revised.

## Key details  
- **Engine fleet**: 36 QSK95‑G9 (large), 3 QSX15‑G9 (small); all equipped with DPFs.  
- **Emissions standards**: PM10 target 0.01 g/bhp‑hr; analysis uses 0.015 g/bhp‑hr for conservatism, evaluated at 50 hrs/yr per CEC direction.  
- **Construction period**: 6‑1‑26 to 12‑1‑27 (17 months).  
- **Dispersion options**: Urban coefficients used for all HRA and criteria pollutant files; rural option was corrected.  
- **Risk calculations**: Updated cancer risk at MEIS to 2.41E‑6, at MEIW to 5.10E‑6; PMI cancer risk set to 4.98E‑5.  
- **VOC emissions**: Corrected monthly average from 577.6 lbs to 517.6 lbs; yearly and hourly values adjusted accordingly.
