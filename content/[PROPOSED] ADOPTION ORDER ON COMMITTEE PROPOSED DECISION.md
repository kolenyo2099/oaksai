## Summary  
- [[California Energy Commission]] (CEC) adopts the Committee Proposed Decision on November 24 2021.  
- The decision grants a Small Power Plant Exemption to SV1, LLC for the [[Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility]].  
- The facility will house 36 × 3.25‑MW reciprocating generators and three 500‑kW standby diesel units at Great Oaks Boulevard, San Jose.  
- CEC orders the exemption effective December 8 2021 and requires filing of a Notice of Determination within five business days.  

## Key details  
- **Project**: Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility (Backup Generators + Data Center).  
- **Capacity**: 36 × 3.25 MW = 117 MW reciprocating; plus three 500‑kW diesel generators, total generating capacity not to exceed 100 MW per order.  
- **Location**: 123, 127, and 131 Great Oaks Boulevard, [[San Jose]], California.  
- **Applicant**: SV1, LLC (applicant for exemption).  
- **Decision date**: November 24 2021 (Committee Proposed Decision); adoption effective December 8 2021.  
- **Regulatory basis**: Public Resources Code §§21000 et seq., 25541; CEC power plant licensing process.  
- **Findings**: No substantial adverse impact on environment or energy resources from demolition, construction, and operation activities.  

## Claims & attribution  
- *Claimed by the CEC*: The facility will not exceed 100 MW generating capacity.  
- *Attribution*: Statement appears in the adopted Commission Decision; no independent verification provided in the text.  
- *Uncertainty*: No external confirmation of the exemption status beyond the CEC order.
