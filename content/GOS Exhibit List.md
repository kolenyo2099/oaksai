## Summary  
- [[California Energy Commission]] staff identified all documents that will be used as exhibits in the evidentiary hearing for the [[Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility]] small power plant exemption.  
- The list includes a Final Environmental Impact Report, staff opening testimony, an addendum to the report, and several other supporting documents filed under docket 20‑SPPE‑01.  
- Documents cover environmental impacts such as GHG, air quality, biological resources, cultural resources, geology, soils (paleontology), hazards, hazardous materials, hydrology, water quality, noise, and transportation.  
- The City of San Jose’s acceptance of the MMRP and mitigation compliance is listed as Exhibit 203.  
- Staff responses to committee questions and an intervenor’s reply testimony are also scheduled for hearing.  

## Key details  
- **Docket Number:** 20‑SPPE‑01 (TN 239584)  
- **Project Title:** Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility Small Power Plant Exemption  
- **Filing Date:** 9/2/2021 3:54 PM (submission by Pam Fredieu, California Energy Commission staff)  
- **Exhibit Numbers & Titles**

  - 203 239476 – City of San Jose – Acceptance of MMRP and Mitigation Compliance (same subject areas as above)
