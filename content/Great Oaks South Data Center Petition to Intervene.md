## Summary  
- [[Robert Sarvey]] petitions to intervene in the approval of the [[Great Oaks South Backup Generating Station]] (Project 20‑SPPE‑01).  
- The petition argues that large data centers in California, especially in [[Santa Clara]] and [[San Jose]], rely on diesel‑fired backup generators lacking emissions controls.  
- Sarvey claims these generators cause significant air‑quality harm and lack GHG limits during emergency operation.  
- He urges the State Energy Resources Conservation and Development Commission to adopt cleaner backup technology and model emissions under emergency conditions.  
- The petition requests full participation in topics such as Air Quality, Energy Resources, GHG Emissions, and Environmental Justice.

## Key details  
- **Project title:** Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility Small Power Plant Exemption (TN #238679).  
- **Docket number:** 20‑SPPE‑01.  
- **Filing date:** July 6 2021, 8:44 AM.  
- **Location of petitioner’s address:** 501 W. Grant Line Rd., Tracy, CA 95376.  
- **Petitioner's position:** Advocate for elimination of diesel backup generators and promotion of cleaner alternatives.  
- **Capacity claim:** The generating station is said to exceed 100 MW.  
- **Requested topics for intervention:** Alternatives, Air Quality, Energy Resources, GHG Emissions, Hazardous Materials, Public Health, Reliability, Efficiency, Traffic & Transportation, Biology, Environmental Justice, Utilities & Service systems.  

## Claims & attribution  
- **Claimed by** [[Robert Sarvey]]: diesel backup generators used at data centers have no TAC or GHG limits during emergencies.  
- **Unverified:** The assertion that the project’s emissions will offset “worthless paper emission reduction credits” and that the local air‑quality decline is directly linked to this project.  
- **Source of mitigation measure support:** Sarvey cites CEC Staff’s mitigation measure GHG‑1, though no independent verification is provided in the petition.

DOCKETED Docket Number: 20-SPPE-01 Project Title: Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility Small Power Plant Exemption TN #: 238679 Document Title: Great Oaks South Data Center Petition to Intervene Description: Sarvey Great Oaks South Data Center Petition to Intervene Filer: Robert Sarvey Organization: Robert Sarvey Submitter Role: Intervenor Submission Date: 7/6/2021 8:44:21 AM Docketed Date: 7/6/2021 State of California State Energy Resources Conservation and Development Commission In the matter of: Great Oaks South Backup Docket 20-SPPE-01 Generating Station Robert Sarvey’s Petition to intervene Robert Sarvey hereby petitions to intervene in the Great Oaks South Backup Generating Station. Section 1207 of the Commission’s Rules of Practice and Procedure allow that, “Any person may file with the Docket Unit or the presiding committee member a petition to intervene in any proceeding. The petition shall set forth the grounds for the intervention, the position and interest of the petitioner in the proceeding, the extent to which the petitioner desires to participate in the proceedings, and the name, address, and telephone number of the petitioner.” Petitioner is concerned about the plethora of large data centers being located in California and specifically in Santa Clara and San Jose. All of these data centers utilize massive amounts of diesel fired back-up generators which have no criteria, TAC, or GHG emissions limits during emergency operation. Despite the known environmental damage from employing multiple back up diesel generators government agencies continue to allow there use in large quantities at data centers when less damaging back up generation technology is available. Air Quality in the BAAQMD airshed is declining after years of improvement from the impacts of global warming and wildfires and the additional criteria pollutants from the project which will occur in the future are offset by worthless paper emission reduction credits sometimes generated decades before. The local and regional impacts from the project’s emissions from just the testing of the back-up generators are significant. Petitioner intervenes in support of CEC Staff’s mitigation measure GHG-1. Petitioner wishes to advocate for cleaner technology and the elimination of diesel backup generation for the project. Petitioner believes the generating capacity of the Great South Oaks Generating Station exceeds 100 MW. Petitioner will advocate for the modeling of the project’s emissions in emergency mode. The availability of energy resources to serve this project is in dispute in light of the state’s electrical emergencies. If granted intervention petitioner would like to participate in the topics of Alternatives, Air Quality, Energy Resources, GHG Emissions, Hazardous Materials. Public Health, Reliability, Efficiency, Traffic and Transportation, Biology, Environmental Justice, and Utilities and Service systems. Petitioner intends to participate fully in the proceeding including testimony, briefing and cross-examination of the parties’ witnesses. Respectfully submitted, Robert Sarvey 501 W. Grant Line Rd.
