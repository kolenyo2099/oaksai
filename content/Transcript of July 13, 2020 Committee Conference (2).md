## Summary  
- The California Energy Commission (CEC) held a remote committee conference on July 13, 2020 to discuss the **Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility** small‑power plant exemption application.  
- Applicants [[Masoud Zafaripour]] and [[Scott Galati]] represent SV1, LLC, a subsidiary of Equinix, proposing 36 × 3 MW generators plus life‑safety units for the Great Oaks South Data Center in [[San Jose, California]].  
- The application was filed on March 19, 2020; the CEC docket number is **20-SPPE-01** and the conference notice posted July 2, 2020.  
- Committee members were [[Karen Douglas]] (presiding) and [[David Hochschild]] (associate); hearing officer was [[Susan Cochran]] (acting for Rebecca Westmore).  
- Public comment included representatives from the Bay Area Air Quality Management District, California Air Resources Board, and City of San Jose.

## Key details  
- **Project scope**: 36 three‑megawatt generators to support data‑center load; 3 half‑megawatt units for life safety (one per building).  
- **Estimated demand**: 99 MW across the three buildings.  
- **Regulatory context**: Small Power Plant Exemption (SPPE) under CEC rules, consistent with Governor Newsom Executive Orders N‑25‑20 and N‑29‑20 on COVID‑19 measures.  
- **Participants**:  
  - Committee: [[Karen Douglas]], [[David Hochschild]];  
  - Hearing officer: [[Susan Cochran]] (acting for Rebecca Westmore);  
  - CEC staff: [[Lisa Worrall]] (project manager), [[Lisa DeCarlo]] (staff counsel), Jared Babula;  
  - Advisors: Kourtney Vaccaro, Eli Harland, Le‑Quyen Nguyen, John Hilliard;  
  - Public agencies: Jakub Zielkiewicz (Bay Area Air Quality Management District), Pippin Mader (California Air Resources Board), Meenaxi Raval (City of San Jose).  

## Claims & attribution
