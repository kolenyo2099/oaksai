## Summary
- [[Terry Schumacher]] objects to the proposed power plant on Great Oaks Blvd.  
- Claims the site is too close to residential homes, being only ~2000 ft away from his property.  
- States the state failed to monitor air‑quality changes promised after a previous plant was built in Coyote Valley.  
- Asserts the new plant would severely reduce property values and is unnecessary.  

## Key details
- **Project title:** Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility Small Power Plant Exemption TN #233753.  
- **Docket number:** 20‑SPPE‑01.  
- **Submission date:** July 4, 2020 1:36 PM (public comment).  
- **Location:** Proposed site on Great Oaks Blvd, adjacent to residential area in Tennessee.  
- **Distance from complainant’s home:** ~2000 ft.
