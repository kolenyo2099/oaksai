## Summary  
- The California Energy Commission assigned [[RALPH LEE]] and [[LINDA BARRERA]] as hearing officers for the small power plant exemption proceeding on the proposed [[Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility]].  
- Assignment was issued by Chair [[DAVID HOCHSCHILD]] of the State Energy Resources Conservation and Development Commission.  
- The memo references docket number 20‑SPPE‑01 and is dated August 6, 2020.
