## Summary
- [[David Keyon]], Principal Planner at the [[City of San Jose]] confirmed via email that the city’s 2030 [[Greenhouse Gas Reduction Strategy (GHGRS)]] was approved by the council on 17 November 2020.  
- The conversation, logged in a California Energy Commission (CEC) ROC, centers on the status of the GHGRS and its implications for the [[Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility]] small‑power‑plant exemption (SPPE).  
- CEC staff, notably [[Tao Jiang]], Air Resources Engineer, are coordinating with SJCE to explore providing 100 % carbon‑free electricity under the Total Green program for the facility.  
- Several City of San José Clean Energy staff (e.g., Zach Struyk, Kate Ziemba) participated in the email chain, indicating collaboration across planning, code enforcement and clean‑energy departments.  
- The exchange includes technical details such as proposed mitigation measure MM GHG‑1, which requires project owners to purchase Total Green electricity from SJCE.

## Key details
- **Project**: Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility (small‑power plant exemption 20‑SPPE‑01).  
- **Timeline**: GHGRS approved 17 Nov 2020; email exchange dated 1 Dec 2020 and 16–19 Jul 2021.  
- **Participants**:  
  - [[David Keyon]] – Principal Planner, City of San José Planning, Building & Code Enforcement.  
  - [[Tao Jiang]] – Air Resources Engineer, CEC Siting, Transmission & Environmental Protection Division.  
  - SJCE representatives (Zach Struyk, Kate Ziemba, etc.).  
- **Mitigation measure**: MM GHG‑1 – participation in the San José Clean Energy Total Green level (100 % carbon‑free electricity).  
- **Stakeholders**: City of San José, California Energy Commission, SJCE.  

## Claims & attribution
- *Claimed by* [[David Keyon]] that the 2030 GHGRS was council‑approved on 17 Nov 2020 (confirmed in email).  
- *Claimed by* CEC staff that it is feasible for SJCE to supply 100 % Total Green electricity to the facility; this claim originates from Zach Struyk’s July 19 email and corroborated by earlier correspondence.  
- *Unverified* details include the final approval status of MM GHG‑1 and the exact timeline for the facility’s construction ramp‑up, as these were only referenced but not finalized in the provided text.
