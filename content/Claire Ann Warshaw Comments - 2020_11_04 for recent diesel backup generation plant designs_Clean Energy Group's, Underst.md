## Summary
- [[Claire Ann Warshaw]] comments on recent Small Power Plant Exemptions (SPPEs) for diesel backup generators in Silicon Valley, noting their environmental impact.  
- Lists several SPPE submissions: Sequoia, Mission College, Walsh, San Jose Data Center, Laurelwood, Lafayette, and Great Oaks South.  
- Highlights the prevalence of diesel Tier 1‑3 engines in new designs despite California’s clean‑energy goals.  
- Recommends the Clean Energy Group’s “Understanding Solar + Storage” guide to promote solar‑battery alternatives.  
- Notes that the guide is part of the Resilient Power Project, funded by multiple foundations.

## Key details
- **SPPE submissions** (2020‑11‑04): Great Oaks South (TN #235518) and others listed above, all in Santa Clara County.  
- **Design concerns**: Diesel generators are noisy, emit pollutants, and may operate during wildfire emergencies, posing public health risks.  
- **Author background**: Warshaw worked as a designer for [[Sacramento Municipal Utility District]] (SMUD).  
- **Clean Energy Group guide**: “Understanding Solar + Storage” released 2020‑10‑20; addresses solar PV with battery storage, sizing, safety, and value.  
- **Resilient Power Project**: Joint effort of Clean Energy Group and Meridian Institute; supported by The JPB Foundation, The Kresge Foundation, Surdna Foundation, Nathan Cummings Foundation, The New York Community Trust, Barr Foundation, and Merck Family Fund.  
- **Funding acknowledgements**: Contributions from Mayra Cruz (Catalyst Miami), Logan Atkinson Burke (Alliance for Affordable Energy), Shelley Robbins (Upstate Forever), Chelsea Barnes (Appalachian Voices), Timna Zemel & Chris Keast (Simpliphi Power), Will Heegard (Footprint Project), Geoff Oxnam (American Microgrid Solutions).

## Claims & attribution
- **Claim**: Diesel backup generators in recent SPPEs contribute significantly to California’s emissions and are counter‑productive to clean‑energy goals.  
  - *Attribution*: Claimed by [[Claire Ann Warshaw]] based on her professional experience and analysis of SPPE submissions.  
- **Claim**: The “Understanding Solar + Storage” guide provides viable alternatives for reliable backup power without diesel.  
  - *Attribution*: Presented by the Clean Energy Group; not independently verified in the provided text but supported by the guide’s content and funding sources.  
- **Uncertainty**: No independent verification of emission reductions or cost comparisons is presented; claims rely on Warshaw’s expertise and the guide’s recommendations.
