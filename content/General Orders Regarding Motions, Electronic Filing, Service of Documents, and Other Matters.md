## Summary
- The [[California Energy Commission]] (CEC) issued general orders for the Small Power Plant Exemption (SPPE) application of the [[Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility]].
- Orders establish motion filing requirements, e‑filing deadlines, and service procedures governed by CEC regulations and presiding member authority.
- All documents must be submitted via the CEC’s e‑filing system; paper‑copy waivers are allowed under specific conditions.
- The Committee was chaired by [[Karen Douglas]] (Commissioner & Presiding Member) with [[David Hochschild]] as Associate Member.
- Contact points for questions include Hearing Officers [[Susan Cochran]] and [[Rebecca Westmore]], and the CEC Public Advisor [[Noemí O. Gallardo]].

## Key details
- **Docket number**: 20‑SPPE‑01  
- **Project title**: Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility Small Power Plant Exemption  
- **Filing deadline rule**: Documents filed after 5:00 p.m. Pacific Time or on non‑business days are deemed filed the next business day; default deadline is 5:00 p.m. PT unless otherwise specified.
- **E‑filing system**: All parties must use CEC’s e‑filing portal; approved documents appear on the project web page (https://ww2.energy.ca.gov/sitingcases/greatoakssouth/).
- **Confidentiality**: Documents with an approved confidentiality designation are withheld from public view per CEC regulations.
- **Paper‑copy waiver**: Presiding Member may grant a waiver if a party lacks efficient electronic access; paper copies must be mailed to the Docket Unit and converted to e‑filing format.
- **Proof of Service list**: Maintained by the Hearing and Policy Unit; available via the project web page.

## Claims & attribution
- The orders claim that all procedural rules are consistent with CEC regulations (§§ 1203, 1211) and the Committee’s authority (§ 1208).
- These claims are made by the CEC through its official order dated July 8, 2020; no independent verification is provided in the text.
- The requirement for a 14‑day response to motions is attributed to § 1211 of the regulations.
