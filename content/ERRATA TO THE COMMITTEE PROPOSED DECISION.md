## Summary
- [[Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility]] received a Small Power Plant Exemption; the Committee issued an errata to its proposed decision on December 8 2021.  
- Corrections update the Draft EIR public review dates and comment sources, clarify NOx emission findings, and affirm the Final EIR’s adequacy in evaluating alternatives.  
- The California Energy Commission (CEC) staff released the Draft Environmental Impact Report (EIR) on May 21 2021, initiating a 45‑day review that ended July 6 2021.  
- Comments were received from [[BAAQMD]], the Applicant, the City of San Jose, and Enchanted Rock; the City filed an extension request on July 13 2021.  
- The errata confirms that routine operation emissions would not exceed BAAQMD thresholds or pose significant environmental impacts.

## Key details
- **Project**: [[Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility]] – a small power plant backup generator in Santa Clara County, California.  
- **Docket number**: 20‑SPPE‑01 (filed December 8 2021).  
- **Regulatory body**: State Energy Resources Conservation and Development Commission of the State of California; decision approved by Commissioner Karen Douglas and Chair David Hochschild.  
- **EIR timeline**: Draft EIR released May 21 2021; public review 45 days ending July 6 2021.  
- **Commenters**: [[Claire A. Warshaw]] (BAAQMD), the Applicant, City of San Jose, Enchanted Rock.  
- **NOx findings**: Final EIR concludes NOx emissions are fully offset via permitting with BAAQMD; routine operation would not exceed thresholds or cause significant impacts.  
- **Alternative analysis**: Final EIR deemed adequate to inform approval decision.

## Claims & attribution
- The errata states that the CEC staff revised text on pages 10, 29, and 65 of the Committee Proposed Decision to reflect accurate dates, comment sources, and environmental findings.  
- These claims are attributed directly to the CEC’s published errata document; no independent verification is provided in the article.
