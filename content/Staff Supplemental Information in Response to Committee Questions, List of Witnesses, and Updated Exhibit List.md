## Summary
- [[California Energy Commission]] staff responded to four committee questions regarding noise, ambient conditions, baseline data timing, and greenhouse gas mitigation for the Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility.
- Noise construction period estimated at ~34 months, with less noisy phases totaling ~17 months; overall project duration ~81 months.
- Staff claimed operational noise would be below city limits (55 dBA Leq) and that ambient daytime levels averaged 63 dBA Leq, making the project's contribution less than the threshold.
- The applicant cited COVID‑19 traffic reductions as a reason 2020 data would be unrepresentative; staff noted 2016 measurements remain valid for current conditions.
- Staff affirmed the City of San Jose’s 2030 Greenhouse Gas Reduction Strategy is a qualified climate action plan under CEQA, mitigating GHG impacts to less than significant levels.

## Key details
- **Project**: Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility (20‑SPPE‑01), Small Power Plant Exemption TN #239784.
- **Noise construction timeline**: ~34 months total; 32 months trenching/building/paving, 2 months site prep/grading; three phases each <12 months; ~17 months less noisy period; ~30 months between phases.
- **Ambient noise data**: Collected in 2016 at LT‑1 (~100 ft from site); average daytime 63 dBA Leq over three days (Jan 26–28, 2016). Project maximum simultaneous operation 47 dBA Leq (Exhibit 24).
- **City threshold**: 55 dBA Leq for residential receptors per Table 20.135.
- **GHG mitigation**: City of San Jose’s 2030 Greenhouse Gas Reduction Strategy qualifies under CEQA §15183.5(b); staff cites FEIR p. 4.8‑16 and TN 239063.

## Claims & attribution
- *Staff claim*: Construction noise will be below city limits; project contribution < ambient levels; baseline data from 2016 remain representative; GHG strategy qualifies as a mitigation measure.
- *Applicant claim*: COVID‑19 traffic reductions render 2020 noise data unrepresentative; ambient levels during pandemic were lower but temporary.
- Uncertainty: Staff does not present independent verification of the 2016 measurements’ representativeness beyond citing them; no external audit of GHG strategy compliance provided.
