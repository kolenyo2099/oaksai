## Summary  
- [[Mimi Patterson]] emails [[Paul Brownell]] of [[Equinix]] requesting conceptual elevations and detailed drawings for a proposed project in San Jose.  
- She cites that [[China Mobile]] completed an Environmental Impact Report (EIR) with fewer generators at a site farther from the neighborhood, questioning why it was required but not required for Equinix.  
- The email chain discusses scheduling a community Zoom call on 23 Oct 2020 to address resident concerns and provide project details.  
- [[Equinix]] provides links to its Small Power Plant Exemption (SPPE) application documents hosted by the California Energy Commission (CEC).  
- The discussion references the City of San Jose Department of Planning, Building and Code Enforcement’s role in approving Equinix’s initial 2015‑17 application for three large data centers and 21 generators without an EIR.  

## Key details  
- Project: Three large data centers with a small power plant comprising 21 generators at the Great Oaks South site (San Jose, CA).  
- Dates: Email sent 21 Oct 2020; proposed Zoom call scheduled 23 Oct 2020 at 1 pm.  
- Documents linked by Equinix include status conference presentation and SPPE application PDFs covering maps, project description, noise, environmental and air quality assessments.  
- China Mobile’s EIR documents are attached as examples of smaller data center plans with fewer generators located farther from the neighborhood.  

## Claims & attribution  
- **Claimed by Mimi Patterson (uncertain):**  
  - China Mobile was required to complete an EIR despite building only one data center with fewer generators, whereas Equinix was not required for its larger project.  
  - The City of San Jose Department of Planning, Building and Code Enforcement did not require Equinix to provide conceptual elevations in the initial application.  
- **Claimed by Paul Brownell (verified):**  
  - All requested documents are public and available through the CEC e‑filing portal.  
  - The community Zoom call will be held on 23 Oct 2020, subject to confirmation from Councilmember Jimenez’s office.
