## Summary
- [[Nick Renna]] recommends the Environmental Impact Report (EIR) for [[Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility]] should not be limited to that single site.  
- The EIR must also consider nearby data centers, specifically a [[China Mobile]] facility under construction directly across the street.  
- Three operational [[Equinix]] data centers and one nearly completed center lie within half a mile of the proposed site.  
- The comment was submitted on 25 October 2020 as part of docket 20‑SPPE‑01.

## Key details
- **Project**: Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility (small power plant exemption).  
- **Docket number**: 20‑SPPE‑01, TN # 235416.  
- **Comment date**: 25 Oct 2020 9:57 AM; docketed 26 Oct 2020.  
- **Proposed site location**: adjacent to a China Mobile data center under construction; within 0.5 mi of three operational Equinix data centers and one nearly completed Equinix data center.  
- **Recommendation**: Expand EIR scope to include environmental impacts of all nearby data centers.

## Claims & attribution
- [[Nick Renna]] claims that the current EIR is too narrow, focusing only on the Great Oaks South site.  
- He asserts that the proximity of multiple data centers (China Mobile and Equinix) warrants a broader assessment.  
- No independent verification or corroborating sources are provided in the text; the claim originates solely from Renna’s submission.

DOCKETED Docket Number: 20-SPPE-01 Project Title: Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility Small Power Plant Exemption TN #: 235416 Document Title: Nick Renna Comments - EIR SCOPE GOSBGF Description: N/A Filer: System Organization: Nick Renna Submitter Role: Public Submission Date: 10/25/2020 9:57:11 AM Docketed Date: 10/26/2020 Comment Received From: Nick Renna Submitted On: 10/25/2020 Docket Number: 20-SPPE-01 EIR SCOPE GOSBGF I recommend that the EIR for the GREAT OAKS SOUTH BACKUP GENERATING FACILITY not be restricted to evaluating the environmental impact of this one site. There are three Equinix data centers currently operational and one more nearly completed just a half a mile from this proposed site. Additionally, directly across the street from this proposed site is a China Mobile data center under construction. An EIR needs to take into account the environmental impact of all the data centers in immediate location of each other.
