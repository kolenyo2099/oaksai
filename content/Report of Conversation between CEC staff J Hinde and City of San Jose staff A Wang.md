## Summary
- [[California Energy Commission]] staff Jeanine Hinde and City of San Jose planner Angela Wang discussed the status of the city’s review of an amended special use permit for the [[Great Oaks South Data Center Project]].
- The original special use permit (SP15‑031) issued 01/23/2017 remains active; an amendment (SPA15‑031‑01) was submitted 03/03/2020 and is under review.
- Hinde requested clarification on building height, FAR compliance, and design approvals for rooftop equipment, which Wang’s office addressed.
- The conversation notes that the project conforms to height limits of 50 ft in IP districts and meets FAR requirements (0.7 FAR vs max 12).
- Emails attached include the original SP15‑031 permit and subsequent correspondence regarding design adjustments.

## Key details
- **Project**: Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility, a data center complex with three two‑story buildings (~579 k sq ft total).  
- **Permit timeline**: Original SP15‑031 approved 01/23/2017; amendment SPA15‑031‑01 submitted 03/03/2020; city review pending third submittal.  
- **Building heights**: Approved height 50 ft; current designs show precast at 49 ft, parapet/coping 53.25 ft, screen wall 63.25 ft, penthouse 72.25 ft—within IP district limits (250 ft).  
- **FAR**: Project FAR 0.7 (547,050 sq ft/784,080 sq ft), well below maximums of 10.0 (IP) and 12.0 (TEC); no minimum FAR specified.  
- **Design approvals**: Rooftop screen wall and penthouse reviewed under major Permit Adjustment AD‑18‑1179; architectural design changes approved.
