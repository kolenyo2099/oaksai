## Summary  
- [[Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility]] is the subject of a Small Power Plant Exemption (SPPE) docket 20‑SPPE‑01.  
- The California Energy Commission (CEC) issued a notice on 15 June 2021 setting the deadline for public comments to **6 July 2021**.  
- Public comment can be submitted in writing electronically or via email; oral testimony is allowed at the pre‑hearing and hearing sessions.  
- A public hearing was scheduled for **7 September 2021** (pre‑hearing) and a fact‑based hearing on **13 September 2021**.  
- The notice referenced earlier CEC guidance documents and lists of staff such as [[Susan Cochran]] and [[Ralph Lee]] who are the committee members.

## Key details  
- **Docket number:** 20‑SPPE‑01 (Project: Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility).  
- **Filer:** [[Ngoc Tran]], California Energy Commission.  
- **Committee members:** [[Susan Cochran]] (Staff, CEC) and [[Ralph Lee]] (Staff, CEC).  
- **Deadline for written comments:** 6 July 2021 (45 days after the draft EIR was released on 21 May 2021).  
- **Public hearing dates:** 7 September 2021 (pre‑hearing) and 13 September 2021 (fact‑based hearing).  
- **Comment submission methods:**  
  - Online via CEC e‑filing portal: https://efiling.energy.ca.gov/Ecomment/Ecomment.aspx?docketnumber=20-SPPE-01  
  - Email to docket@energy.ca.gov with docket number and project name in the subject line.  
- **Accessibility services:** Spanish, Vietnamese, or other language interpretation and reasonable accommodation requests must be made by 5 pm at least five days before the hearing.  
- **Staff who can intervene:** Individuals designated as "intervening staff" under California Code of Regulations, title 20, §1212(a).  

## Claims & attribution  
- The notice claims that public participation is encouraged and that comments may be made orally or in writing; this claim originates from the CEC’s own communication.  
- It asserts that the hearing dates are **planned** for 7 September (pre‑hearing) and 13 September (fact‑based); these dates are subject to change, as noted by the agency.  
- The document references prior CEC guidance documents (TN 233123, TN 235279, etc.) but does not independently verify their applicability; those references are taken at face value from the notice.
