## Summary  
- [[Bill Dunmyer]] writes to [[California Energy Commission]] staff about the proposed [[Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility]].  
- He criticises lack of notice and proximity to residential areas, citing potential noise, emissions, and water use concerns.  
- The facility would host 30 diesel generators (21 approved in 2017 plus 9 added in 2020) with a total fuel capacity of 331 200 k gallons.  
- [[PG&E]] and the City are alleged to have considered alternative sites on Monterey Road but chose this location near homes.  
- Dunmyer threatens legal action, class‑action lawsuits, and media outreach if the project proceeds.

## Key details  
- **Project**: Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility (Small Power Plant Exemption TN #234335).  
- **Generators**: 30 primary diesel units (21 approved 2017 + 9 added 2020) plus 6 redundant units.  
- **Fuel storage**: 9,200 k gal per generator; 110,400 k gal for 12 generators; total 331,200 k gal when operational.  
- **Water use**: ~112 million gallons annually per building (343 acres of water usage).  
- **Noise & emissions**: Not quantified in the comment but flagged as concerns.  
- **Hazardous material handling**: Tanker trucks carry 8,500 k gal diesel; spill basins; no ground‑water safeguards mentioned.  
- **Air quality measures**: Twice‑daily watering of exposed surfaces, covered haul trucks, street sweeping, limited idling (5 min).  
- **Location context**: Site described as an empty field near residential homes, 100 ft from a street; alternative sites on Monterey Road noted.
