## Summary  
- [[California Energy Commission]] staff submitted an addendum to the Final Environmental Impact Report (FEIR) for the Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility on 18 August 2021.  
- The addendum makes minor edits to **MM GHG‑1** and clarifies that the project will participate in the **San Jose Clean Energy (SJCE)** Total Green level, i.e., 100 % carbon‑free electricity.  
- An updated Mitigation Monitoring and Reporting Program (MMRP) was attached and approved by the City of San Jose to meet its 2030 Greenhouse Gas Reduction Strategy.  
- The changes do not alter the FEIR conclusion that the project would not result in any significant adverse environmental impacts.  

## Key details  
- **Project**: Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility, a small power plant exemption (TN #239361).  
- **Addendum ID**: 20‑SPPE‑01, filed on 18 August 2021 at 2:24 PM.  
- **MM GHG‑1 change**: Owner must participate in SJCE Total Green level or a comparable clean‑energy program; this does not affect overall environmental conclusions.  
- **MMRP contents**: Measures for air quality, fugitive dust control, exhaust emissions (Tier 4/3/2 diesel equipment), and biological resources (nesting bird surveys, construction scheduling).  
- **City of San Jose role**: Lead Agency responsible for implementing the MMRP; has reviewed and approved all edits.
