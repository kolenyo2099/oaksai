## Summary
- [[California Energy Commission]] issued a memorandum setting key dates for the Small Power Plant Exemption (SPPE) process for the [[Great Oaks South]] backup generating facility.  
- The deadline to file an Intervenor Petition is **6 July 2021**, calculated 45 days after the draft Environmental Impact Report was published on 21 May 2021.  
- A pre‑hearing conference is scheduled for **7 September 2021** at 10:00 a.m., followed by a probative hearing on **13 September 2021** at 10:00 a.m.; both dates are subject to change.  
- Local, state, tribal and federal agencies, as well as members of the public, may participate without being intervenors.  
- Comments can be submitted electronically via the docket portal or by email to docket@energy.ca.gov.

## Key details
- **Project**: Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility Small Power Plant Exemption (SPPE) – Docket No. 20‑SPPE‑01.  
- **Draft EIR publication date**: 21 May 2021; **Intervenor Petition deadline**: 6 July 2021.  
- **Pre‑hearing conference**: 7 Sep 2021, 10:00 a.m.; **Probative hearing**: 13 Sep 2021, 10:00 a.m.
