## Summary
- Conversation between [[Joseph Hughes]] (Air Resources Supervisor, California Energy Commission) and [[Jakub Zielkiewicz]] (Advanced Projects Advisor, Bay Area Air Quality Management District) about non‑testing/non‑maintenance diesel engine operations at data centers.  
- Data was included in BAAQMD’s comment letter (TN235803) on the Notice of Preparation for the Environmental Impact Report of the Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility.  
- CEC staff requested clarifications; BAAQMD provided updated datasets and responses to specific questions.  
- The exchange involved 45 data center facilities, with 66 total permitted in the Bay Area.  
- Detailed engine operation statistics (number of engines, capacity) were shared for each facility.

## Key details
- Project: Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility (Docket 20‑SPPE‑01).  
- Dates of email exchange: December 4, 2020; December 18, 2020.  
- Data set includes 45 facilities, 66 permitted data centers in Bay Area.  
- Engine counts and capacities per facility are listed (e.g., Facility 1: 10 engines, 20 MW total capacity).  
- BAAQMD’s comment letter referenced as TN235803; the conversation is documented in TN237631.  
- Participants: [[Joseph Hughes]] (CEC), [[Jakub Zielkiewicz]] (BAAQMD), and additional BAAQMD staff [[Pamela Leong]], [[Henry Hilken]], [[Alexander Crockett]].  

## Claims & attribution
- **Claim**: The data set reflects non‑testing/non‑maintenance diesel engine operations at the specified facilities.  
  - *Attribution*: CEC staff’s email from December 4, 2020 and subsequent BAAQMD responses.  
- **Claim**: 66 data center facilities are permitted under BAAQMD jurisdiction; 45 provided data.  
  - *Attribution*: Joseph Hughes’ statement in the email chain.  
- **Unconfirmed**: Whether all engines listed operated for the entire capacity duration or only partially; detailed operational logs not included in the text.
