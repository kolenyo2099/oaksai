## Summary  
- [[SV1, LLC]] (a wholly owned subsidiary of [[Equinix, LLC]]) filed a Small Power Plant Exemption (SPPE) application for the [[Great Oaks South]] backup power plant on 19 March 2020.  
- The California Energy Commission (CEC) scheduled a hearing on 13 July 2020 after issuing notices and requiring staff reports by 7 July and applicant responses by 10 July.  
- Staff proposed an Environmental Impact Report (EIR) rather than an Interim Statement/Mitigation Notice (IS/MND), citing concerns from the California Air Resources Board (CARB) and the Bay Area Air Quality Management District (BAAQMD).  
- The CEC board ultimately approved the EIR requirement, rejecting the applicant’s request for an IS/MND.  
- Community outreach was encouraged; staff was directed to provide a signage plan by 10 November 2020.

## Key details  
- **Project**: Great Oaks South backup generating facility (SPPE docket 20‑SPPE‑01).  
- **Applicant**: [[SV1, LLC]] (Equinix subsidiary).  
- **Regulator**: California Energy Commission (CEC), formerly the Energy Resources Development and Conservation Board.  
- **Key dates**:  
  - 19 Mar 2020 – application filed.  
  - 02 Jul 2020 – CEC notice issued; hearing scheduled for 13 Jul.  
  - 07 Jul 2020 – staff submitted preliminary status report.  
  - 10 Jul 2020 – applicant responded.  
  - 07 Aug 2020 – updated status report; EIR proposed.  
  - 31 Aug 2020 – notice for hearing on 23 Sep; requested status report by 10 Sep.  
  - 23 Sep 2020 – hearing; staff and applicant disagreed on necessity of an EIR.  
  - 15 Oct 2020 – staff’s decision to require an EIR issued (signed by [[Karen Douglas]] and [[David Hochschild]]).  
- **Regulatory guidance**: CEC relied on the California Environmental Quality Act (CEQA) to justify the EIR, emphasizing public information needs and potential large‑scale impacts.  
- **Community engagement**: Staff encouraged community input; a signage plan was requested for the project site.
