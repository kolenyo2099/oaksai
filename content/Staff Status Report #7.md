## Summary
- [[California Energy Commission]] staff issued Status Report #7 for the Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility (Project 20‑SPPE‑01) on March 10, 2021.  
- The report confirms that the project is still awaiting an updated description that will include Tier 4 compliant backup generators and associated impacts, expected to be docketed by March 10, 2021.  
- Staff notes progress on environmental impact review (EIR) areas not affected by the Tier 4 conversion, with only the traffic analysis remaining incomplete.  
- The draft EIR is still scheduled for publication in line with the Committee’s current order.  

## Key details
- **Project**: Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility Small Power Plant Exemption (20‑SPPE‑01).  
- **Docket number**: 237116, filed by [[Lisa Worrall]] of the California Energy Commission.  
- **Submission date**: March 10, 2021 at 8:34 AM.  
- **Stakeholders**: Commissioner [[Karen Douglas]] (Presiding Member), Chair [[David Hochschild]] (Associate Member).  
- **Location**: 1516 Ninth Street, Sacramento, CA 95814‑5512.  
- **Status**: Awaiting updated project description with Tier 4 backup generators; traffic analysis incomplete per Status Report #6.  

## Claims & attribution
- [[California Energy Commission]] staff claims the project remains on schedule for draft EIR publication, *subject to* the pending traffic analysis and Tier 4 revisions.  
- The claim that the updated project description will be docketed by March 10, 2021 is attributed to the applicant’s Status Report #3.
