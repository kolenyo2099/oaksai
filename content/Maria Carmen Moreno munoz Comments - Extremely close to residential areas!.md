## Summary  
- [[Maria Carmen Moreno munoz]] objects to the proposed location of the Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility, citing its proximity to residential areas.  
- She argues that Pacific Gas & Electric’s (PG&E) unreliability could keep diesel generators running for days, increasing pollutant exposure.  
- The comment highlights how smoke from wildfires can stagnate on hills, worsening air quality near the site.  
- Moreno recommends relocating the plant closer to I‑101, farther from residents and hill slopes where exhaust fumes may pool.  

## Key details  
- **Project**: Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility (Small Power Plant Exemption TN #233752).  
- **Docket Number**: 20-SPPE-01.  
- **Comment Date**: July 5, 2020 at 1:32 AM.  
- **Location Concern**: Site lies extremely close to residential areas and hills that trap smoke; proposed relocation would be near I‑101 highway.  
- **Equipment Mentioned**: Diesel generators (small power plant).  
- **Health Risk**: Accumulation of pollutants could threaten community health along the hills.  

## Claims & attribution  
- *Claim*: The site is “extremely close to residential areas.” — Attributed to [[Maria Carmen Moreno munoz]].  
- *Claim*: PG&E has shown unreliability last year, potentially keeping generators on for several days. — Attributed to Moreno.  
- *Claim*: Wildfire smoke gets caught against hills and stagnates without high wind, leading to pollutant buildup. — Attributed to Moreno.  
- *Claim*: Moving the plant closer to I‑101 would reduce exposure to residents and prevent exhaust pooling. — Attributed to Moreno.

DOCKETED Docket Number: 20-SPPE-01 Project Title: Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility Small Power Plant Exemption TN #: 233752 Document Title: Maria Carmen Moreno munoz Comments - Extremely close to residential areas! Description: N/A Filer: System Organization: Maria Carmen Moreno munoz Submitter Role: Public Submission Date: 7/5/2020 1:32:29 AM Docketed Date: 7/6/2020 Comment Received From: Maria Carmen Moreno munoz Submitted On: 7/5/2020 Docket Number: 20-SPPE-01 Extremely close to residential areas! The proposed location is extremely close to residential areas and with the unreliability that PG&E has shown last year to keep the power on; these could run for several days at a time. It is my experience that in the past during wildfires the smoke gets caught up against the hills and stagnates unless there is a high wind to push it away. If this situation occurs than the amount of pollutants from these diesel generators would accumulate and pose a threat to the health of the whole community living along the hills. Please reconsider moving this closer to 101 where it is further from residents and is not so close to the hills where the exhaust fumes can pool for long periods of time.
