## Summary
- The California Energy Commission (CEC) scheduled a pre‑hearing and evidence‑based hearing for the Small Power Plant Exemption (SPPE) application of Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility.  
- Pre‑hearing on **Wednesday, September 7, 2021** at 10 am; evidence‑based hearing on **Tuesday, September 13, 2021** at 10 am.  
- The hearings will be held remotely in compliance with California Executive Order N‑08‑21 due to COVID‑19.  
- The applicant is SV1, LLC, a wholly owned subsidiary of Equinix, LLC, proposing a data center and backup power plant near San Jose, CA.  
- The backup plant would have 36 diesel generators (3.25 MW each) totaling ~99 MW to support the data center’s maximum demand.  

## Key details
- **Project**: Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility – a small power plant exemption under CEC rule §25541.  
- **Applicant**: SV1, LLC (Equinix subsidiary).  
- **Location**: 123, 127, 131 Great Oaks Ave., San Jose, CA; ~18 acres.  
- **Backup generators**: 36 units × 3.25 MW = 117 MW capacity; 99 MW usable for data center load.  
- **Safety generators**: 3 units × 0.5 MW each (one per building).  
- **CEC hearing dates**: Pre‑hearing – 7 Sep 2021, 10 am; Evidence‑based hearing – 13 Sep 2021, 10 am.  
- **Remote participation**: Via links provided in the CEC notice; public comment and observation allowed.
