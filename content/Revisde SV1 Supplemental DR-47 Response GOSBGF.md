## Summary
- [[SV1, LLC]] submits a supplemental response to Data Request 47 for the Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility (GOSBGF) Small Power Plant Exemption application.
- The response details construction emissions estimates for three proposed 21 kV underground feeder trenches: Via Del Oro, Santa Teresa Route 1, and Santa Teresa Route 2.
- Emissions are evaluated using CalEEMod; operational emissions are not calculated as the lines will be underground.
- Estimated CO₂e emissions per route: 30.3 MT (Del Oro), 50.3 MT (Santa Teresa 1), and 52.0 MT (Santa Teresa 2).
- Sidewalk demolition/reconstruction associated with Route 2 adds 51 hauling trips for concrete and debris removal.

## Key details
- **Project**: Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility, a data‑center campus in [[Santa Clara County]].
- **Feeder routes**:
  - Via Del Oro: 2,920 ft trench, 4 ft depth, 2 ft width; 1.5 project months (2022 start).
  - Santa Teresa Route 1: 5,671 ft trench, same dimensions; 2.5 months (2024 start).
  - Santa Teresa Route 2: 5,671 ft trench; 2.5 months (2027 start).
- **Construction equipment** per trench: 1 excavator, 1 backhoe, 1 conduit truck, 2 other trucks, 8 workers/day.
- **Emissions (CalEEMod)**:
  - Del Oro: CO₂e = 30.3 MT; NOx = 0.00447 t/period; SOₓ = 0.0194 t/period.
  - Santa Teresa 1: CO₂e = 50.3 MT; NOx = 0.00735 t/period; SOₓ = 0.0315 t/period.
  - Santa Teresa 2: CO₂e = 52.0 MT; NOx = 0.00739 t/period; SOₓ = 0.0351 t/period.
- **Sidewalk project** (Route 2): 36 concrete haul trips (285 yd³) + 15 debris haul trips.
