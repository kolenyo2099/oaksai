## Summary

- [[Wesley Dyer]] of the California Air Resources Board forwarded an email from community member [[Mimi Patterson]] to [[Ralph Lee]], a co‑hearing officer for the Great Oaks SPPE application.  
- Mimi claims she was not properly notified about the proposed Great Oaks South data center and small power plant, citing lack of public outreach since 2015.  
- She accuses [[Equinix]]’s attorney [[Scott Galati]] of pressuring CA agencies to expedite reports and dismissing a full Environmental Impact Report (EIR).  
- Mimi requests that the City of San Jose Planning and Development reconsider the application process and pursue a new EIR.  
- The email references several individuals: [[Jakub Zielkiewicz]], [[Angela Wang]], and mentions Mimi’s educational background at Pace University School of Law and Cornell University.

## Key details

- Project docket: 20‑SPPE‑01, Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility, Small Power Plant Exemption.  
- Email sent from [[Wesley Dyer]] (Attorney, California Air Resources Board) to [[Ralph Lee]] (Co‑Hearing Officer).  
- Mimi Patterson is a resident of South San Jose, California, near the proposed site and a community member in the area of Via Del Oro.  
- Mimi cites a 2’ x 3’ sign posted on Via Del Oro in 2015 that was never erected; no public notices or outreach were conducted through 2017.  
- Mimi alleges that [[Equinix]]’s project involved adding fifteen generators and significant design changes since the original application.  
- She calls for a full EIR rather than a Mitigated Negative Declaration (MND) initially granted by the City of San Jose Planning and Development Department.

Had there been public outreach, we would be in a very different scenario today with this proposed project. I am reaching out to you today for your assistance and guidance in this matter. I believe a full Environmental Impact Report (EIR) is a more prudent and responsible process than simply a Mitigated Negative Declaration (MND) that the City of San Jose Planning and Development initially granted. I know Equinix's attorney, Scott Galati, incessantly tried to dissuade you and Jakub Zielkiewicz on the zoom wedinar call by attempting to expedite your reports and input by deeming your analysis redundant and unnecessary. On the contrary, your expertise and analysis are crucial in finding that these data centers and small power plant will have extreme and dire negative impacts on our community. I hope you will complete a full EIR. Also, with the additional fifteen (15) generators, huge time lapse since initial application and the magnitude of the design change, is there a way to recommend to the City of San Jose Planning and Development Department and the applicant to complete a new application process?
