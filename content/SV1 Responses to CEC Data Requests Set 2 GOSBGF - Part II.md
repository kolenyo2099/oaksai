## Summary  
- The document is a response to California Energy Commission data requests for the **Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility** (GOSBGF) located on Great Oaks Boulevard/Santa Teresa Boulevard in San Jose, CA.  
- It contains an **EDR Aerial Photo Decade Package**, providing one historical aerial photograph per decade from 1939 to 2012 at a scale of 1" = 500'.  
- The report also includes an **EDR City Directory Abstract** covering occupant information for addresses around the target property from 1922‑2013, sourced mainly from R. L. Polk & Co., Pacific Bell, and other telephone directories.  
- Key findings list several adjacent properties (e.g., 160 Great Oaks Blvd, 6580 Del Oro) and note current occupants or historical uses such as **DAS TE K CORP**, **Advanced Storage Technology**, and **Actrix Computer Corp**.  
- The document is issued by **Scott Galati** on behalf of **DayZenLLC** and was submitted to the California Energy Commission on 5 / 17/2020.

## Key details  
- **Target property:** Great Oaks Boulevard/Santa Teresa Boulevard, San Jose, CA 95119.  
- **Aerial photo years & sources:** 1939, 1948, 1950, 1956, 1968, 1974, 1982, 1993, 1998 (USGS/DOQQ), 2005, 2006, 2009, 2010, 2012 (USDA/NAIP).  
- **Scale:** 1" = 500' for all photographs.  
- **City Directory Abstract coverage:** 1922‑2013, with occupant data at five‑year intervals.  
- **Adjacent property addresses researched:** 6580 Del Oro, 160 Great Oaks Blvd, 268 Great Oaks Dr, 6577 Santa Teresa Blvd.  
- **Notable adjacent occupants (historical):** DAS TE K CORP, Advanced Storage Technology, Actrix Computer Corp, Baccarat Electronics Inc., Corvus Systems Inc., ZENTEC Corp.  
- **Document metadata:** Docket number 20‑SPPE‑01, Project title: Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility Small Power Plant Exemption, TN #: 233005‑2.
