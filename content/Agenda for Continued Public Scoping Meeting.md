## Summary
- The California Energy Commission held a remote public scoping meeting for the [[Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility]] on December 11, 2020 via Zoom.
- Meeting focused on technical areas with potential significant impacts, including air quality, greenhouse gas emissions, and alternative technologies.
- Participants could join online or by telephone; meeting ID 977‑5674‑3271 with password 636115 was provided.
- Discussion topics included use of NOx offsets, BAAQMD significance thresholds, participation in the City of San Jose’s clean energy program, emergency operation GHG estimates, and alternative options such as fuel cell technology and battery storage.
