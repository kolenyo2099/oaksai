## Summary  
- [[California Energy Commission]] (CEC) rescheduled the evidentiary hearing for the Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility to **September 21, 2021** at 10:00 a.m.  
- The hearing will be conducted remotely via Zoom in line with Executive Order N‑08‑21 on COVID‑19 pandemic response.  
- The application seeks a small power plant exemption for a facility with capacity of 50 MW or more.  
- Committee members include [[Karen Douglas]] (Commissioner and Presiding Member) and [[David Hochschild]] (Chair and Associate Member).  
- Participants, including the applicant, CEC staff, and intervenor [[Robert Sarvey]], confirmed availability for the new date during a pre‑hearing conference on September 7.  

## Key details  
- **Docket**: 20‑SPPE‑01 (Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility SPPE).  
- **Applicant**: SV1, LLC, wholly owned subsidiary of [[Equinix]] LLC.  
- **Pre‑hearing Conference**: September 7, 2021; hearing officer noted full Committee could not meet on September 13, leading to reschedule.  
- **Hearing Format**: Remote Zoom meeting (Webinar ID 914‑9436‑0781, Password GOSPPE@10).  
- **Closed Session Provision**: Committee may adjourn to a closed session under Government Code §11126(c)(3) for deliberations.  

## Claims & attribution  
- The CEC *claims* that the hearing is rescheduled due to logistical constraints and participant availability (confirmed by applicant, staff, intervenor).  
- The notice *attributed* the decision to the Committee’s agreement reached verbally during the pre‑hearing conference; no independent verification beyond the transcript pending.
