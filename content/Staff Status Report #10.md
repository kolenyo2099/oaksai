## Summary
- [[California Energy Commission]] staff released **Draft Environmental Impact Report (EIR)** for the [[Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility Small Power Plant Exemption (20-SPPE-01)]], published on May 21, 2021.  
- A Notice of Availability was sent to a wide mailing list, including responsible agencies and residents within 1,000 ft of the site and 500 ft of project linears; it was also translated into Spanish and Vietnamese.  
- The public comment period ran from May 21 to July 6, 2021, during which **no comments** were received.  
- The status report complies with a Committee order requiring monthly reports starting September 10, 2020.  

## Key details
- **Project:** Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility Small Power Plant Exemption (20-SPPE-01).  
- **Draft EIR Publication Date:** May 21, 2021.  
- **Notice of Availability Distribution:** Sent to responsible and trustee agencies, state agencies, owners/occupants adjacent to the site, property owners within 1,000 ft, property owners within 500 ft of project linears, environmental and community organizations; translated into Spanish and Vietnamese for residents in the same proximity zones.  
- **Public Comment Period:** 45 days, May 21–July 6, 2021.  
- **Comments Received:** None reported to date.  
- **Reporting Requirement:** Committee Conference & Related Orders (Sept 23, 2020) mandated status reports by the 10th of each month starting Sept 10, 2020.
