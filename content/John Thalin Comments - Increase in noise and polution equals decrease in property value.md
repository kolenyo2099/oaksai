## Summary

- [[John Thalin]] alleges a large project near his home will reduce air quality, increase noise, and lower property values.  
- The project is described as an industrial complex that has not been presented to the [[City Council]].  
- He claims no public notice was given in accordance with guidelines from the [[City of San Jose Planning and Development Dept.]].  
- Thalin demands that all activities related to the project be halted.  
- The project is referred to as the [[Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility]].

## Key details

- **Project**: Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility, a small power plant exemption (TN #: 235316).  
- **Location**: Near Thalin’s home in a quiet neighborhood of San Jose.  
- **Timeline**: Project slated to begin shortly before the comment was filed on 10/19/2020.  
- **Regulatory gap**: Thalin claims it has never been presented to the City Council for approval and no public notice was issued per City of San Jose Planning and Development Dept. guidelines.
