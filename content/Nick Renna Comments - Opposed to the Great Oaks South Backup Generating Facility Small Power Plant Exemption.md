## Summary  
- [[Nick Renna]] opposes the Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility Small Power Plant Exemption.  
- The proposed site is located just across his street on Santa Teresa Blvd in San Jose.  
- He cites proximity (800 ft) and a surrounding population of ~3,000 homes within one mile as concerns.  
- Environmental worries include noise, traffic, and pollution; potential negative impact on property values.  
- A coalition of over 200 residents is forming to oppose the project, with plans to meet the district councilman and invite the Mayor of San Jose.

## Key details  
- Project: Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility Small Power Plant Exemption (Docket #20‑SPPE‑01).  
- Location: Santa Teresa Blvd, San Jose, California.  
- Proximity: Nick Renna’s backyard 800 ft from the proposed site; 13 homes line Santa Teresa Blvd facing it.  
- Nearby population: ~3,000 homes within a one‑mile radius.  
- Coalition: >200 residents expressed interest; scheduled meeting with district councilman and invitation to Mayor of San Jose.
