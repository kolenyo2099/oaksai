## Summary
- [[SV1, LLC]] submitted supplemental responses to the California Energy Commission’s Data Request 58 regarding the Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility (project number 19‑SPPE‑03).  
- The submission includes utility line drawings from the PG&E Santa Teresa Substation and on‑site one‑line diagrams for the facility.  
- [[DayZenLLC]] is listed as the filer, with [[Scott Galati]] as the applicant representative.  
- The documents detail a planned 33 MW power distribution per building site, with conduit and conductor specifications to be provided by PG&E.  
- Architectural and engineering drawings are supplied by multiple firms: [[SHEEHAN NAGLE HARTRAY ARCHITECTS]], [[StringhamDesign]], and [[Southland Industries]].

## Key details
- **Project**: Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility (19‑SPPE‑03) – a small power plant exemption in San Jose, CA.  
- **Submitter**: SV1, LLC (also listed as DayZenLLC).  
- **Representative**: Scott Galati.  
- **Submission Date**: 7 / 15 / 2020 09:35:24 AM.  
- **Utility Connection**: PG&E Santa Teresa Substation via 6‑inch conduits; primary enclosure dimensions 4′ × 8′ 6″.  
- **Power Capacity**: 33 MW planned for each building site (total capacity not specified).  
- **Drawing References**: E001, E002, E101–E106, E108, E110, etc., with detailed single‑line diagrams for SV12, SV18, and SV19.  
- **Architectural Firms**: SHEEHAN NAGLE HARTRAY ARCHITECTS; StringhamDesign; Southland Industries.  
- **Location of Documents**: 639 Front Street, San Francisco, CA 94111 (SV1 LLC office) and 130 E Randolph St, Chicago, IL 60601 (DayZenLLC office).  

## Claims & attribution
- **Claimed by SV1, LLC**: The facility will be connected to PG&E’s Santa Teresa Substation with the specified conduit layout.  
- **Claimed by DayZenLLC**: All drawings comply with Data Request 58 requirements and reflect current design plans.  
- **Unverified**: No independent audit or third‑party verification of the 33 MW capacity per building site is provided in the submission.
