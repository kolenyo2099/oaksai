## Summary

- The City of [[San José]] Planning, Building and Code Enforcement Department confirms concurrence with the Mitigation Monitoring and Reporting Program (MMRP) for the Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility.  
- City officials will act as enforcement agency to ensure compliance by the Great Oaks South Data Center.  
- The letter references edits to MM GHG‑1 dated 18 August 2021 and acknowledges the Environmental Impact Report SCH 2020100431.  
- Contact information is provided for Adam Petersen, contract environmental planner.  

## Key details

- **Project**: Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility (Small Power Plant Exemption).  
- **Document**: Acceptance of MMRP and Mitigation Compliance; docket number 20‑SPPE‑01.  
- **Date submitted**: 25 August 2021, 4:10 PM.  
- **City address**: 200 E. Santa Clara Street, 3rd FL, San José, CA 95113.  
- **California Energy Commission contact**: Lisa Worrall, Senior Environmental Planner, Sacramento, CA.  
- **MMRP edits**: MM GHG‑1 revised on 18 August 2021.

The Planning Division oversees the City's General Plan and zoning policies; review applications for planning permits; and engage the community on development proposals. The following comments are based on our review of the Mitigation Monitoring and Reporting Program (MMRP). The City concurs with the MMRP, including the edits to MM GHG-1, dated August 18, 2021. Further, the City will serve as the enforcement agency for the MMRP, ensuring the Great Oaks South Data Center complies with all requirements outlined in the MMRP. Thank you for including the City of San José in the environmental review process.
