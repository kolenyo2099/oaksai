## Summary  
- [[Lisa Campbell]] expresses concern about the safety impact of the Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility on local air quality and hazardous material storage.  
- She cites a document by her neighbor, [[Nick Renna]], supporting similar worries.  
- The project’s diesel generators are reportedly causing rolling blackouts in the neighborhood.  
- Campbell requests studies that confirm the site’s safety and seeks contact with responsible parties.  

## Key details  
- Project: Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility (Small Power Plant Exemption TN #234333).  
- Docket Number: 20‑SPPE‑01, approved in 2017.  
- Safety issue raised on 2020‑08‑17 by [[Lisa Campbell]] via public submission.  
- Claim that the project was not notified to residents within 1000 ft, only 500 ft (error admitted by Patrick from the planning group).  
- Rolling blackouts reported as of the date of comment.

DOCKETED Docket Number: 20-SPPE-01 Project Title: Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility Small Power Plant Exemption TN #: 234333 Document Title: Lisa Campbell Comments - Great Oaks South Project - Safety Description: N/A Filer: System Organization: Lisa Campbell Submitter Role: Public Submission Date: 8/17/2020 4:37:15 PM Docketed Date: 8/17/2020 Comment Received From: Lisa Campbell Submitted On: 8/17/2020 Docket Number: 20-SPPE-01 Great Oaks South Project 20SPEE01 Safety I am very concerned about what this project will do to the safety of our neighborhood, specifically air quality and hazardous material being stored so close to a residential area. My neighbor, Nick Renna has completed a very thorough document outlining concerns. I am in total agreement. I would like to see the studies that suggest this is safe in our neighborhood? We attended a ZOOM call last Friday with our councilman Sergio and members of the various groups responsible for managing this project. Patrick from the planning group said this was in fact not sent out to within 1000 feet, which is what they normally do, but 500 feet, which he said was an error, so when this project was approved in 2017, none of us in the immediate area where notified. As I write this, we are experiencing rolling black outs, which only furthers our concerns about having all of theses diesel generators running in our neighborhood. We would like our concerns addresses, please let me knw who can speak to this. Thank you!
