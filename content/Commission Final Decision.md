## Summary  
- The California Energy Commission (CEC) granted a Small Power Plant Exemption for the Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility, approved on December 8 2021.  
- The project includes 36 3.25‑MW standby diesel generators and three 500‑kW life‑safety generators at addresses 123, 127, and 131 Great Oaks Boulevard in San Jose, California.  
- Total nameplate capacity is capped at 99 MW; the CEC found no substantial adverse environmental or energy‑resource impacts.  
- The exemption allows the facility to operate without full CEC power‑plant licensing under Public Resources Code §25541.  
- The decision was adopted by the CEC commissioners, with a formal order and subsequent filing of a Notice of Determination to the State Clearinghouse.  

## Key details  
- **Project name:** Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility (Backup Generators) + Great Oaks South Data Center.  
- **Location:** 123, 127, 131 Great Oaks Boulevard, San Jose, California; ~18 acres in an Industrial Park zone.  
- **Generators:** 36 standby diesel generators × 3.25 MW each; 3 life‑safety diesel generators × 0.50 MW each.  
- **Capacity:** Collective nameplate >99 MW, but operational limit set at 99 MW to avoid exceeding the 100‑MW threshold for SPPE.  
- **Regulatory context:** Exemption under Public Resources Code §25541; CEC serves as lead agency under CEQA for SPPEs.  
- **Decision date:** December 8 2021 (effective); hearing on November 24 2021, errata December 8 2021.  
- **Commissioners:** David Hochschild (Chair), Siva Gunda (Vice Chair), Karen Douglas, Patty Monahan, Andrew McAllister, Ralph Lee, Susan Cochran.  

## Claims & attribution  
- **Claimed by CEC:** The Backup Generators will not exceed 100 MW; no substantial adverse impact on environment or energy resources.  
- **Attribution:** Findings are based on the hearing record and Final Environmental Impact Report (FEIR) held in the CEC docket.  
- **Uncertainty:** None reported; statements are presented as adopted findings of the commission.
