## Summary
- [[Mimi Patterson]] writes to [[District CouncilMember Sergio Jimenez]] about the [[Equinix]] project in San Jose.  
- She accuses Equinix of neglecting public notice and community outreach for a massive data center and small power plant with thirty‑six generators.  
- The email references a recorded Zoom meeting involving city planners, a CEC Public Advisor, and local council members.  
- Patterson urges the council to intervene against what she calls “code violations” and negative environmental impacts.  
- She offers community meetings (in person or virtual) to discuss concerns.

## Key details
- Project: Great Oaks South Bay Equinix Data Centers and Small Power Plant.  
- Generators: thirty‑six (36).  
- Date of email: September 30, 2020 at 7:20 AM.  
- Email recipients: [[District2@sanjoseca.gov]], Helen Chapman, Maribel Villarreal, Lucas Ramirez, Laura Nguyen, Kimberly Hernandez; CC list includes Patricia Sheehan, Nick Renna, Bill Dunmyer, Kandl Ferguson, Pat Mundt, Janelle Casanave, Shannon Kaloczy, Louise Renna, Lisa Campbell, Rosemary Avalos.  
- Recorded Zoom meeting mentioned: with City of San Jose Planning and Development representatives, CEC Public Advisor RoseMary Avalos, and council members Nick Renna and Lisa Campbell.
