## Summary  
- [[California Energy Commission]] issued Staff Status Report #9 for the Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility Small Power Plant Exemption (20‑SPPE‑01).  
- The report notes progress on the draft Environmental Impact Report (EIR) and targets publication around May 17, 2021.  
- Final data responses were received up to March 18, 2021 (TN 237205).  
- Staff acknowledges potential delay beyond the scheduled publication date.  

## Key details  
- **Project**: Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility Small Power Plant Exemption (20‑SPPE‑01) – TN #237767.  
- **Reporting entity**: [[California Energy Commission]]; senior environmental planner Lisa Worrall (1516 Ninth Street, Sacramento, CA).  
- **Committee order**: In the Committee Conference and Related Orders dated September 23, 2020, parties were directed to file monthly status reports starting September 10, 2020.  
- **Draft EIR schedule**: Targeted for publication week of May 17, 2021; final date may shift if data processing is incomplete.  
- **Last data response**: March 18, 2021 (TN 237205).  

## Claims & attribution  
- [[California Energy Commission]] claims the draft EIR will be published by the week of May 17, 2021.  
- Staff acknowledges that the publication date could be delayed; this is an unconfirmed statement pending final data receipt.
