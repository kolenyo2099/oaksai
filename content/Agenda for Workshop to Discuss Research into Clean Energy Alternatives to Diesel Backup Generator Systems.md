## Summary
- Workshop held January 21, 2021 at 10 am–4 pm via Zoom to explore clean alternatives to diesel backup generators.  
- Key speakers from the California Energy Commission (CEC), California Public Utilities Commission (CPUC), CAISO, CARB and industry partners presented current research and future needs.  
- Panels highlighted technology options such as battery storage, fuel cells, hydrogen, and renewable gas, with input from companies like Bloom Energy, Microsoft, Plug Power, and Form Energy.  
- Participants were encouraged to submit written comments through the “Submit Comments (19‑ERDD‑01)” link.
