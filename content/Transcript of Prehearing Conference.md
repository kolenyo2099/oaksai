## Summary
- The **California Energy Commission** held a pre‑hearing conference on September 7 2021 to discuss the application for a **Small Power Plant Exemption (SPPE)** for the **Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility**.  
- The applicant is **SV1, LLC**, a wholly owned subsidiary of **Equinix**, seeking to construct and operate the **Great Oaks South Data Center** and related facilities at 123‑131 Great Oaks Boulevard in **San Jose**.  
- Participants included commissioners, advisors, hearing officers, staff attorneys, project managers, an intervener (**Robert Sarvey**), and public agency representatives; no public comments were received.  
- The conference reviewed readiness for the upcoming evidentiary hearing, identified potential witnesses and exhibits, and clarified procedural matters.

## Key details
- **Docket number:** 20‑SPPE‑01 (Small Power Plant Exemption).  
- **Applicant:** SV1, LLC (Equinix subsidiary).  
- **Project scope:** Three two‑story data center buildings (~547 000 sq ft) plus a backup generating facility.  
- **Site location:** City of San Jose, California; 123, 127, 131 Great Oaks Boulevard.  
- **Meeting format:** Remote via Zoom (Governor Newsom Executive Order N‑08‑21).  
- **Key staff:** Hearing officers Ralph Lee and Susan Cochran; Staff attorneys Lisa DeCarlo and Lisa Worral; Project manager Lisa Worrall.  
- **Intervenor:** Robert Sarvey.  
- **Public agencies present:** None reported; no Native American tribe representatives.

## Claims & attribution
- The Commission claims the application is *ready* for an evidentiary hearing, pending discussion of disputed subject areas (unverified in transcript).  
- SV1, LLC asserts it has complied with all filing requirements and seeks exemption from the 50‑MW licensing threshold.  
- No independent verification of the applicant’s compliance status appears in the provided text.
