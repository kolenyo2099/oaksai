## Summary  
- [[California Energy Commission]] (CEC) granted intervention to [[Robert Sarvey]] in the Small Power Plant Exemption application for the [[Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility]].  
- The CEC committee allowed Sarvey to participate on direct, indirect, and cumulative impacts across 12 topic areas including alternatives, air quality, energy resources, greenhouse gas emissions, public health, hazards, reliability, efficiency, traffic, biological resources, environmental justice, utilities, and service systems.  
- Intervention was granted following a petition filed July 6 2021; no opposition was received by the deadline July 20 2021.  
- The order was signed by Committee members [[Karen Douglas]] (Commissioner & Presiding Member) on July 30 2021 and [[David Hochschild]] (Chair & Associate Member) on July 31 2021.  
- CEC provided procedural guidance, document access links, and contact information for public participation and technical inquiries.

## Key details  
- **Petition filing date:** July 6 2021 by Robert Sarvey (address: 501 W. Grant Line Rd., Tracy, CA).  
- **Project docket number:** 20‑SPPE‑01.  
- **Committee designation date:** May 13 2020; members: Karen Douglas and David Hochschild.  
- **Intervention scope:** Participation in hearings on 12 environmental and operational topics listed above.  
- **Document access:** All project documents available at https://efiling.energy.ca.gov/Lists/DocketLog.aspx?docketnumber=20-SPPE‑01 and the Great Oaks South SPPE web page https://ww2.energy.ca.gov/sitingcases/greatoakssouth/.  
- **Contact points:** Public Advisor Susan Cochran, Hearing Officers Ralph Lee & Susan Cochran, Project Manager Lisa Worrall; phone numbers and emails provided.  
- **CEC address effective Aug 1 2021:** 715 P Street, Sacramento, CA 95814.

## Claims & attribution  
- The CEC *claims* that Sarvey demonstrated a bona fide interest and possession of relevant information (statement in the order).  
- This claim is made by the CEC’s committee; no independent verification provided within the text.  
- The order states intervention is granted at the Committee’s discretion, subject to future modification.
