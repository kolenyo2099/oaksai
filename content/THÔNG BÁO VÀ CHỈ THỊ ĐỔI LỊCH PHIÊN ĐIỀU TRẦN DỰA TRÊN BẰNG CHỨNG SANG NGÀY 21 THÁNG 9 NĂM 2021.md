## Summary  
- [[Energy Commission]] (CEC) announced a change of the “Evidence‑Based Hearing” for the Small Power Plant Exemption (SPPE) application concerning the [[Great Oaks South]] backup power facility.  
- The hearing, originally scheduled for 13 September 2021, was moved to **Wednesday, 21 September 2021 at 10 a.m.** and will be conducted remotely per COVID‑19 order N‑08‑21.  
- CEC designated a committee chaired by [[Karen Douglas]] (Chair & Member‑at‑Large) and led by [[David Hochschild]] (Chair & Associated Member).  
- The notice includes instructions for remote participation via Zoom, with links and ID/password details.  
- Public comments are limited to three minutes per speaker during the hearing; closed‑session deliberations may occur under California Government Code § 11126(c)(3).

## Key details  
- **Docket**: 20‑SPPE‑01 – Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility Small Power Plant Exemption.  
- **Original hearing date**: 13 September 2021 (cancelled).  
- **New hearing date**: 21 September 2021, 10:00 a.m. PST.  
- **Location**: Remote via Zoom; ID 914‑9436‑0781, password GOSPPE@10.

  - [[Karen Douglas]] – Chair & Member‑at‑Large (CEC).

- **Regulatory basis**: California Public Resources Code § 25200; Executive Order N‑08‑21 (COVID‑19).  
- **Public participation**: up to three minutes per comment; Zoom “raise hand” feature recommended.  
- **Contact information**:

  - Hearing staff: Susan Cochran (susan.cochran@energy.ca.gov), Ralph Lee (ralph.lee@energy.ca.gov).
