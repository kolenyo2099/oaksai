## Summary  
- [[Mimi Patterson]] emails the City of San Jose Planning and Development Department about the [[Great Oaks South Bay Equinix Data Center]] and Small Power Plant project.  
- She claims the project was approved without required public notice or community outreach.  
- The email references a recorded Zoom meeting held on 30 September 2020 with City staff including [[Nick Renna]] and [[Lisa Campbell]].  
- Patterson accuses the city and applicant of negligence, code violations, and indifference toward neighborhood concerns.  
- She urges the District Council Member to advocate for residents against the project’s negative impacts.

## Key details  
- **Project**: Great Oaks South Bay Equinix Data Center & Small Power Plant (Great Oaks South).  
- **Location**: Near the proposed site in San Jose, California.  
- **Approval status**: Approved by the city without mandated public notice or outreach.  
- **Stakeholders contacted**:  
  - City of San Jose Planning and Development Department (PD) – recipients of email.  
  - District Council Member (Jimenez) – addressed in message.  
  - Public Advisor’s Office, led by [[RoseMary Avalos]].  
  - Other PD staff: [[Rosalynn Hughey]], [[Robert Manford]], [[Tim Rood]].  
- **Meeting**: Recorded Zoom meeting on 30 September 2020 at 7:20 AM.  
- **Attachments**: PDF titled “Equinix Data Centers and Small Power Plant Great Oaks South Project .pdf”.  

## Claims & attribution

Dear Mr. Jimenez, I recently viewed the recorded zoom meeting regarding the Great Oaks South Bay Equinix Data Center and Small Power Plant project with you and your staff, Nick Renna and Lisa Campbell, the City of San Jose Planning and Development, and RoseMary Avalos, Public Advisor's Office. I appreciate you taking the time to set up the zoom meeting and working with members of our community. I am forwarding you a letter that I emailed to Rosalynn Hughey, Robert Manford, and Tim Rood at the City of San Jose Planning and Development Department. Like many of my neighbors, I am deeply disturbed and angered by the negligence and disregard for our neighborhood and community. This Equinix project was approved without the mandated public notice and community outreach, and we are only now becoming aware of this massive data centers and small power plant project. In addition to the City of San Jose Planning and Development Department's and the applicant's apathy and indifference for our neighborhood, there are obvious code violations with this project. As our District CouncilMember, I am asking you and your office to represent and facilitate our fight against this behemoth project and its negative impact on our community and environment. Please help us protect our families, our neighborhood and our community. My neighbors are willing to meet in person (socially-distanced and with masks) or via zoom or conference call. Please let us know what works best for you and your office. I look forward to hearing from you as soon as possible. Best, Mimi Patterson (408) 807-9269
