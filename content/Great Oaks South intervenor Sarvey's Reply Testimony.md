## Summary  
- [[Robert Sarvey]] contests that the CEC staff omitted analysis of the larger [[Great Oaks Mixed Use Project]] and its 720 residential units in air‑quality modeling.  
- The intervenor argues that emergency operations of backup diesel generators at Great Oaks South are inadequately modeled, citing BAAQMD data showing a ~20% annual outage probability for Santa Clara data centers.  
- Sarvey notes that the Air District had requested emission modeling of non‑testing/non‑maintenance generator use, yet CEC staff declined, claiming such events are unpredictable and unmodelable.  
- The testimony criticizes the acceptance of Tier 4 engines without further emissions analysis, asserting this is unsupported by BAAQMD’s own findings.  
- GHG mitigation proposals from the applicant—enrolling in Equinix Clean Energy Program versus SJCE’s 100% green program—are evaluated, with Sarvey favoring the latter for oversight and reliability benefits.

## Key details  
- **Project**: Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility (Docket 20‑SPPE‑01).  
- **Location**: San Jose, California; part of the larger Great Oaks Mixed Use Project.  
- **Residential/Commercial scope**: up to 720 units and 154,000 sq ft commercial space.  
- **Engine type**: Tier 4 diesel generators (emission standard discussed).  
- **Emergency operations data**: BAAQMD reported 8 outages at 39 Santa Clara centers between Nov 27 2019–Sep 16 2020, implying ~20% annual outage probability.  
- **GHG mitigation options**:  
  - Equinix Clean Energy Program (66% RERCs from Oklahoma/Texas wind farms).  
  - SJCE Clean Energy 100% green program (487 MW contracted renewable generation, 44 % load in 2023).  
- **Alternative technology**: Bloom Energy Fuel Cells (20 MW at SV‑11 near Great Oaks South), noted as not yet proven for large‑scale backup.
