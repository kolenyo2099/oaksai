## Summary
- The [[California Energy Commission (CEC)]] Committee issued a Scheduling Order on October 16, 2020 requiring monthly status reports for the Great Oaks Backup Generating Facility SPPE (20‑SPPE‑01).  
- In SVP Status Report 1 – GOSBGF, the Applicant requested that the Committee affirm that emergency modeling of emissions would be unnecessary.  
- The request was not titled as a motion, violating General Orders that motions must state the relief sought in the title.  
- Consequently, the Committee will not act on the request until it is properly formatted and filed according to the General Orders.  
- Procedural questions should be directed to Hearing Officer [[Susan Burns Cochran]].

## Key details
- **Project**: Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility (SPPE 20‑SPPE‑01).  
- **Committee Scheduling Order**: Filed October 16, 2020; mandates monthly status reports.

DOCKETED Docket Number: 20-SPPE-01 Project Title: Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility Small Power Plant Exemption TN #: 237365 Document Title: Memo Re Making Requests for Committee Action Description: N/A Filer: Ngoc Tran Organization: Energy Commission Hearing Office Submitter Role: Committee Submission Date: 4/2/2021 9:35:58 AM Docketed Date: 4/2/2021 State of California California Natural Resources Agency M e m o r a n d u m To: All Parties and Persons Interested Date: April 2, 2021 in the Great Oaks Backup Generating Facility SPPE (20-SPPE-01) From : Susan Burns Cochran, Hearing Officer (916) 891-8078 Susan.Cochran@energy.ca.gov Subject: MAKING REQUESTS FOR COMMITTEE ACTION On October 16, 2020, the California Energy Commission (CEC) Committee1 filed its Committee Scheduling Order. The Scheduling Order included the requirement that the parties file monthly status reports. In SVP Status Report 1 - GOSBGF, Applicant requested that “the Committee affirm that emergency modeling of emissions would be unnecessary.” We direct the parties’ attention to the General Orders Regarding, Motions, Electronic Filing, Service of Documents, and Other Matters (General Orders). The General Orders state that written motions shall state the motion in the document’s title and provide the basis for the requested relief. The other parties then have the opportunity to respond to any motions filed. Applicant’s request that the Committee affirm whether a particular analysis is required was not included in the title of the document. The failure to do so may deprive other parties of the ability to respond or object to the request. Accordingly, the Committee will not take any action on this request regarding the necessity for analysis of emergency operations change without adherence to the General Orders. While requests do not require a separate filing from a status report, the request must be included in the title of the document in order for it to be considered on the merits by the Committee. If you have any questions of a procedural nature, please contact me as shown above. 1 On May 13, 2020, the CEC appointed a Committee consisting of Karen Douglas, Commissioner and Presiding Member, and David Hochschild, Chair and Associate Member, to preside over this SPPE Application.
