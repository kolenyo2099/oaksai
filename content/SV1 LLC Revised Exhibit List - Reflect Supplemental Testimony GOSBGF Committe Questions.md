## Summary  
- [[SV1, LLC]] submitted a revised exhibit list for the Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility SPPE application on 17 Sep 2021.  
- The list enumerates 35 exhibits, ranging from main applications (232466) to supplemental responses and analyses covering air quality, public health, greenhouse gases, noise, traffic, and emissions.  
- Exhibit volumes vary widely, with the largest being Appendix G (741 pages) and several supplementary data requests totaling over 1 200 pages.  
- The documents include detailed environmental impact information, compliance checklists for [[City of San Jose]] GHGRS, and updated modeling analyses.
