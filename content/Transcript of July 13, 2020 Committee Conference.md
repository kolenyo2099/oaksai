## Summary

- [[California Energy Commission]] held a committee conference on July 13, 2020 to discuss the Small Power Plant Exemption (SPPE) application for the [[Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility]].
- The applicant, [[SV1, LLC]] (a division of [[Equinix]]), seeks exemption to build 36 three‑megawatt generators and three 0.5 MW life‑safety units to supply an uninterruptible power system for the [[Great Oaks South Data Center]] in San Jose.
- The conference was conducted remotely via Zoom, following Governor Newsom’s COVID‑19 executive orders, with a hearing officer acting on behalf of the assigned officer.
- Public agencies present included the [[California Air Resources Board]], [[Bay Area Quality Management District]], and the City of San Jose.
- Proceedings included introductions, presentation of project details, public comment, and a closed‑session discussion.

## Key details

- **Docket number:** 20-SPPE-01
- **Application date:** March 19, 2020
- **Project scope:** Uninterruptible power supply for three data‑center buildings; estimated total demand 99 MW.
- **Generators proposed:** 36 × 3 MW (utility‑grade) + 3 × 0.5 MW (life‑safety).
- **Committee members:** Commissioner [[Karen Douglas]] (presiding), Chair [[David Hochschild]].
- **Hearing officer:** Susan Cochran (acting for Rebecca Westmore).
- **Staff present:** Lisa Worrall, Lisa DeCarlo, Jared Babula, and others from CEC.
- **Public agencies:** Pippin Mader (CARB), Jakub Zielkiewicz (BAAQMND), Meenaxi Raval (City of San Jose).
- **Meeting format:** Remote via Zoom; public comment followed by a closed‑session portion.
