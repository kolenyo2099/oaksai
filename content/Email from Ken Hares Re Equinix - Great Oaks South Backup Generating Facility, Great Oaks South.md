## Summary  
- [[Ken Hares]] of the [[San Ignacio Homeowner's Association]] objects to the construction of the [[Equinix – Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility]].  
- He claims the project violated zoning codes and was approved illegally by the [[San Jose Planning & Development Dept.]] without public hearings or community outreach.  
- Hares lists environmental and safety risks: fire, explosion, fumes, diesel spills, noise, traffic, and pollution near residential areas.  
- The email requests that city officials disapprove the project to protect local livability.  

## Key details  
- Project: Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility (small power plant) located in San Jose, California.  
- Alleged approval by San Jose Planning & Development Dept. without public notice or council vote.  
- Zoning conflict: intended use not permitted for the property per local zoning code.  
- Environmental concerns cited: diesel fuel spills contaminating drinking water; fumes causing health issues; risk of fire/explosion near residential homes across the street.  
- Noise and traffic: construction noise, dirt, congestion, and pollution projected to affect residents for 12 hours a day over seven years.
