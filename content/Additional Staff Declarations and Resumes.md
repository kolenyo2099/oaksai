## Summary
- [[Eric Knight]] and [[Geoffrey Lesh]] submit declarations as staff of the [[California Energy Commission]] supporting the application for a Small Power Plant Exemption for the [[Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility]].
- The declarations affirm that the prepared testimony is accurate, based on independent analysis of the exemption application and related documents.
- Both signatories declare their familiarity with the facts and state they could testify if called.
- The filings are dated 9 March 2021 and were submitted to the commission’s docket (20‑SPPE‑01) for review by committee members.

## Key details
- **Project**: Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility – a small power plant exemption case, docketed as 20‑SPPE‑01.
- **Filer**: Lisa Worrall, California Energy Commission staff.
- **Declarations submitted**:
  - Eric Knight, Energy Resources Specialist III (Managerial) – date of declaration: 9 March 2021.
  - Geoffrey Lesh, Supervising Mechanical Engineer – date of declaration: 9 March 2021.
- **Location referenced**: Sacramento, California (site of filing and commission headquarters).
- **Content of declarations**:
  - Both affirm the validity and accuracy of their testimony regarding the exemption application.
  - Both declare personal familiarity with the facts and readiness to testify.
