## Summary
- [[California Energy Commission]] (CEC) scheduled a committee conference on **23 September 2020** to discuss an exemption request for the Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility.
- The request, submitted by [[SV1, LLC]], a wholly‑owned subsidiary of [[Equinix, LLC]], seeks exemption for a small power plant that would supply backup power to the proposed Great Oaks South Data Center in San José, CA.
- Committee members include [[Karen Douglas]] (Commissioner and Chair) and [[David Hochschild]] (President and Associate Member).
- The conference will be held remotely via Zoom in compliance with Executive Orders N‑25‑20 and N‑29‑20 to mitigate COVID‑19 spread.
- CEC staff intend to prepare an Environmental Impact Report (EIR) for the project, replacing a prior plan for a Mitigated Negative Declaration.

## Key details
- **Project site**: 123–131 Great Oaks Boulevard, San José, CA – approximately 18 acres, undeveloped land.
- **Data Center**: three two‑story buildings totaling ~547,050 sq ft to house servers in secure, climate‑controlled enclosures.
- **Backup Generators**: 36 diesel units (3.25 MW each) arranged in six on‑site yards, providing up to 99 MW of power; 12 per building with redundancy and 0.5 MW safety generators per building for fire suppression and emergency operations.
- **Exemption numbers**: TN 232466 and TN 232467‑1 to 232467‑3.
- **CEC staff memorandum (7 August 2020)** announced the shift from a Mitigated Negative Declaration to an EIR, with notification expected in August 2020 and public outreach scheduled thereafter.
- **Meeting logistics**: remote Zoom session; participants may join via links or by entering meeting ID/password; sessions may be recorded.

## Claims & attribution
- CEC staff claim that preparing an EIR is prudent due to increasing resident concerns (source: memo 7 August 2020).
- The exemption request is presented as a small power plant exemption under California law (publicly available documentation on the CEC website).
- No independent verification of environmental impacts or public sentiment is provided in the document.
