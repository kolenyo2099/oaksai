## Summary
- The California Energy Commission (CEC) closed the evidentiary record for the Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility SPPE and set deadlines for optional post‑hearing briefs.  
- Briefs must be filed by 5 pm on October 5, 2021, limited to 15 pages, and may not introduce new evidence.  
- The Committee granted a petition allowing Robert Sarvey to intervene; the pre‑hearing conference confirmed voluntary brief submission.  
- No new evidence is permitted; briefs should address unresolved factual or legal issues from the evidentiary hearing.  
- Contact information for public participation, procedural queries, and technical inquiries was provided.

## Key details
- **Project**: Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility (part of Great Oaks South Data Center).  
- **Site**: 123‑131 Great Oaks Boulevard, San Jose, California.  
- **Docket number**: 20‑SPPE‑01 (TN 239858).  
- **Decision dates**: Pre‑hearing conference – September 7, 2021; evidentiary hearing – September 21, 2021; order issued – September 27, 2021.  
- **Committee members**: [[Karen Douglas]], Commissioner and Presiding Member; [[David Hochschild]], Chair and Associate Member.  
- **Intervenor**: [[Robert Sarvey]] (granted intervention on August 2, 2021).  
- **Submission deadline**: 5 pm October 5, 2021 (seven business days after the hearing transcript filing).  
- **Brief requirements**: 15‑page limit, ≥12‑point font, no attachments, cite evidence by exhibit/page.
