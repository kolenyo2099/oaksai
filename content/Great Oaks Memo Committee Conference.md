## Summary
- [[California Energy Commission]] (CEC) scheduled a Committee Conference for September 16, 2020 to review the small power plant exemption application for the [[Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility]].
- The conference will discuss CEC staff’s proposal to prepare an environmental impact report (EIR) as the required environmental review document under the California Environmental Quality Act.
- Staff has updated its schedule, adding tasks and comment periods associated with the EIR, following earlier memoranda dated July 7 and August 7, 2020.
- The CEC appointed a committee on May 13, 2020 consisting of [[Karen Douglas]] (Commissioner) and [[David Hochschild]] (Chair).
- Hearing officers for the conference include [[Ralph Lee]] and [[Linda Barrera]], who issued the notice dated August 26, 2020.

## Key details
- **Project**: Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility – small power plant exemption application (TN 232466; TN 232467‑1 through 232467‑3).
- **Docket**: 20-SPPE-01; related documents accessible via the CEC web page and online docket links.
- **Committee Conference**: scheduled for September 16, 2020 at 10:00 a.m. (originally held July 13, 2020).
- **Staff Memoranda**:
  - July 7, 2020 memorandum (TN 233795) identified issues and proposed schedule.
  - August 7, 2020 update (TN 234271) recommended a schedule including an initial study and mitigated negative declaration; now intends to prepare an EIR.
- **Regulatory Context**: CEC is formally the “State Energy Resources Conservation and Development Commission” (Public Resources Code § 25200). The environmental review must comply with the California Environmental Quality Act.

## Claims & attribution
