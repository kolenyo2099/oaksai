## Summary
- [[DayZenLLC]] files SV1 Status Report Number 5 for the Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility under docket 20‑SPPE‑01.  
- The report confirms that all outstanding information required by staff to complete the Draft Environmental Impact Report (EIR) has been submitted, including a revised project description, a Revised Air Quality Analysis reflecting Tier 4 compliant engines, and a final Traffic Analysis addressing City of San Jose comments.  
- SV1 urges staff to finalize and publish the EIR in April 2021.  

## Key details
- **Project**: Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility (Small Power Plant Exemption).  
- **Docket**: 20‑SPPE‑01, TN # 237404.  
- **Submission date**: 4/9/2021 12:12:10 PM.  
- **Filer**: Scott A. Galati, counsel for [[DayZenLLC]] (1720 Park Place Drive, Carmichael, CA).  
- **Required EIR updates**:

  - Final Traffic Analysis incorporating all City of San Jose comments.
