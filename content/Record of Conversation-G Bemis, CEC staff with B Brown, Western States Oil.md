## Summary

- [[California Energy Commission]] (CEC) staff contacted a bulk fuel supplier to assess local availability of renewable diesel for data center backup engines.
- [[Bob Brown]] of [[Western States Oil]] confirmed that [[Neste]] supplies the fuel from its distribution facility at 1790 S Tenth Street, San Jose.
- Approximately 230,000 gallons would be needed initially, with potential annual restocking up to 1 million gallons.
- The incremental cost of renewable diesel is about $2.50–$3.00 per gallon; clean air credits exist for transportation but not for stationary fuel uses.
- Renewable fuel users can claim the “Federal Renewable Fuel Standard Program credit” and “California’s Low‑Carbon Fuel Standard credit,” which roughly offset the cost difference, though these are unlikely to apply to stationary engines.
- Neste may refuse to add red dye, preventing off‑road classification and potentially incurring a $0.66/gal road tax.
