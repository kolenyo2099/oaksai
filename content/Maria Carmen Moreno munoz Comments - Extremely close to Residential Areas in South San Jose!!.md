## Summary  
- [[Maria Carmen Moreno munoz]] objects that the proposed [[Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility]] is situated “extremely close to residential areas” in [[South San Jose]].  
- She cites PG&E’s unreliability last year, suggesting power could remain on for several days.  
- The comment warns that smoke from diesel generators would stagnate against hills unless high wind blows it away, potentially harming community health.  
- Moreno proposes relocating the plant closer to I‑101 to reduce proximity to residents and hilltop exhaust pooling.  

## Key details  
- **Project**: Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility – a small power plant exemption (TN #233751).  
- **Location**: Proposed site in [[South San Jose]], near residential hills and close to the I‑101 corridor.  
- **Concerned equipment**: Diesel generators whose exhaust could accumulate in stagnant air masses.  
- **Regulatory context**: Comment filed under docket 20-SPPE-01 on 5 July 2020 (docketed 6 July 2020).

DOCKETED Docket Number: 20-SPPE-01 Project Title: Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility Small Power Plant Exemption TN #: 233751 Document Title: Maria Carmen Moreno munoz Comments - Extremely close to Residential Areas in South San Jose!! Description: N/A Filer: System Organization: Maria Carmen Moreno munoz Submitter Role: Public Submission Date: 7/5/2020 1:41:12 AM Docketed Date: 7/6/2020 Comment Received From: Maria Carmen Moreno munoz Submitted On: 7/5/2020 Docket Number: 20-SPPE-01 Extremely close to Residential Areas in South San Jose!! The proposed location is extremely close to residential areas and with the unreliability that PG&E has shown last year to keep the power on; these could run for several days at a time. It is my experience that in the past during wildfires the smoke gets caught up against the hills and stagnates unless there is a high wind to push it away. If this situation occurs than the amount of pollutants from these diesel generators would accumulate and pose a threat to the health of the whole community living along the hills. Please reconsider moving this closer to 101 where it is further from residents and is not so close to the hills where the exhaust fumes can pool for long periods of time.
