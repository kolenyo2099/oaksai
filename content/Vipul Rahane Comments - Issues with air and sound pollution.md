## Summary
- [[Vipul Rahane]] comments that the data center at Great Oaks South requires diesel generators.  
- He worries about air and sound pollution from those generators.  
- The facility is located across the street on the other side of Santa Teresa Blvd.  
- He cites PG&E power cuts during fire season as a potential problem for nearby residents.  
- Rahane urges that the project not be approved.

## Key details
- **Project title:** Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility – Small Power Plant Exemption.  
- **Docket number:** 20‑SPPE‑01 (TN # 233755).  
- **Submission date:** July 3, 2020 at 6:35 PM.  
- **Comment received date:** July 6, 2020.  
- **Location:** Santa Teresa Blvd., across from the data center.  
- **Key concern:** Use of diesel generators for backup power.
