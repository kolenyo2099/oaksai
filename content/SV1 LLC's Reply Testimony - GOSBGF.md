## Summary

- [[Masoud Zafaripour]] submits sworn testimony on greenhouse gas emissions for the [[Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility]] (GOSBGF) small‑power plant exemption application.
- The testimony addresses modifications to mitigation measure **MM GHG‑1** in the Staff Addendum to the Final Environmental Impact Report (FEIR) for the project.
- Zafaripour, a construction director with [[Equinix Operating Company]], claims his review confirms that the updated mitigation measure will comply with the San Jose GHG Reduction Strategy Plan adopted 2020.
- He declares all facts in the testimony are true and correct under penalty of perjury.
- The declaration was executed in **San Jose, CA** on August 25, 2021.

## Key details

- **Project**: Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility (GOSBGF) – a small‑power plant backup facility linked to the Great Oaks South Data Center (GOSDC).
- **Regulator**: California Energy Commission (CEC), docket 20‑SPPE‑1.
- **Mitigation Measure**: MM GHG‑1, modified in the Staff Addendum (TN 239361) to the FEIR.
- **Compliance claim**: Updated measure will meet the San Jose GHG Reduction Strategy Plan (2020).
- **Testimony author**: Masoud Zafaripour, Director of Construction at Equinix Operating Company, 11‑year tenure, 28 years industry experience.
- **Filing party**: SV1 LLC, applicant; filing date 8/25/2021.
- **Location of execution**: San Jose, CA.
