## Summary  
- [[Robert Sarvey]] files a prehearing conference statement for the [[Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility]] (Project 20‑SPPE‑01).  
- All subject areas are deemed ready for evidentiary hearing; testimony will be submitted in writing.  
- Disputed issues include emergency operations modeling, cumulative impacts analysis, and GHG emissions enrollment in the [[San Jose Clean Energy 100% GHG emission free program]].  
- Witnesses proposed: [[Robert Sarvey]] (Air Quality & GHG), [[Joseph Hughes]], [[Steven Kerr]], [[Michael Lisenbee]], [[Bruce Frandson]].  
- Exhibits listed cover reply testimony, BAAQMD comments, and environmental impact reports.  

## Key details  
- **Project**: Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility (Small Power Plant Exemption TN 239568).  
- **Date filed**: 31 Aug 2021, 3:42 PM.  
- **Location of filer**: 501 W. Grant Line Rd., [[Tracy]], CA 95376.  
- **Regulatory body**: California State Energy Resources Conservation and Development Commission (SEC).  
- **Subject areas ready**: Air Quality, GHG emissions, Alternatives.  
- **Disputed subject areas**:  
  - *Air Quality*: emergency operations modeling required.  
  - *Cumulative Impacts*: analysis of project plus [[Santa Theresa Substation]] and adjacent [[Great Oaks Mixed Use Project]].  
  - *GHG Emissions*: enrollment in the [[San Jose Clean Energy 100% GHG emission free program]] demanded.  
  - *Alternatives*: fuel cells or natural gas considered superior to diesel engines.  
- **Witness schedule**:  
  - Robert Sarvey – written testimony, 5 min.  
  - BAAQMD staff – 30 min on emergency operations, 20 min on GHG‑1 & cumulative impacts (Joseph Hughes).  
  - Applicant witnesses – 10 min each on emergency operations modeling, GHG‑1, and alternatives.  
- **Exhibits**: TN 239449 reply testimony; BAAQMD comments (TN 235803, TN 238700); environmental impact reports (SCH# 2013032047, SCH# 20090720906).
