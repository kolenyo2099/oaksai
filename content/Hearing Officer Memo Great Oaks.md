## Summary
- [[Ralph Lee]] and [[Linda Barrera]], Hearing Officers, issued a memo on January 8 2021 directing parties in the Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility SPPE (20‑SPPE‑01) to file status reports by the 10th of each month.  
- The memo references a Committee Conference Notice dated August 31 2020 that set this reporting schedule, confirmed by orders filed on October 16 2020 and November ?? 2020.  
- If parties have not filed as required by January 8 2021, they must submit reports no later than January 15 2021.  
- The California Energy Commission appointed a committee on May 13 2020 to oversee the application (TN 233123).  
- Committee members include [[Karen Douglas]] (Commissioner) and [[David Hochschild]] (Chair).

## Key details
- **Docket number**: 20‑SPPE‑01 (Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility SPPE Application).  
- **Project title**: Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility Small Power Plant Exemption.  
- **Filer**: Patricia Carlos, California Energy Commission staff.  
- **Submission dates**: Memo issued January 8 2021 at 4:44 PM; status reports due by the 10th of each month starting September 10 2020.  
- **Committee conference notice**: August 31 2020.  
- **Order confirmations**: October 16 2020 (TN 234539, p. 4) and November ?? 2020 (TN 235279, p. 6).  
- **Reporting deadline if missed**: January 15 2021.
