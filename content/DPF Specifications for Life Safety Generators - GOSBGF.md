## Summary
- [[Cummins]] engine model **500 DFEK QSX15-G9** specified for the Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility.  
- Single unit with 500 kW power rating, 480 V 3‑phase electrical output, and 1,800 RPM operating speed.  
- Equipped with a [[RYPOS]] model RH410‑L active filter housing, 304 stainless steel construction, and critical‑grade acoustical attenuation (25–35 dBA).  
- Emission performance meets CARB Level 3+ standards: CO 0.4 gm/bhp‑hr, NOx 4.3 gm/bhp‑hr, PM10 0.10 gm/bhp‑hr (≥85 % reduction to 0.01 gm/bhp‑hr).  
- Documentation filed by [[DayZenLLC]] under docket 20‑SPPE‑01 on September 30, 2020.

## Key details
- **Engine**: Cummins 500 DFEK QSX15-G9, 1 unit, 500 kW, 480 V 3‑phase, 1,800 RPM.  
- **Fuel & Lubrication**: Diesel fuel; lube oil spec ≤0.5 % wt sulfated ash; consumption per Cummins specs.  
- **Filter System**: RYPOS RH410‑L (part TBD), active filter with diesel oxidation catalyst, 304 stainless steel housing.  
- **Acoustics**: Critical‑grade attenuation 25–35 dBA.  
- **Power Requirements**: 480 V 3‑phase; max load 8 kW, average 4 kW.  
- **Emission Summary (CARB Level 3+)**:
  - CO: 0.4 gm/bhp‑hr (≥85 % reduction), target 0.06 gm/bhp‑hr TBD.  
  - NOx + NMHC: 4.3 gm/bhp‑hr, N/A.  
  - PM10: 0.10 gm/bhp‑hr (≥85 % reduction to 0.01 gm/bhp‑hr).
