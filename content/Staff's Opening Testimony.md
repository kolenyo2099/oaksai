## Summary
- [[California Energy Commission]] staff submitted opening testimony for the [[Great Oaks South Backup Generating Facility]] small power plant exemption proceeding (20‑SPPE‑01).  
- Testimony includes a Final Environmental Impact Report and declarations from staff members on hydrology, water quality, utilities, service systems, hazards, and hazardous materials.  
- The City of San Jose is reviewing the proposed Mitigation Monitoring and Reporting Program (MMRP) to ensure it meets city needs; an agreed MMRP will be filed by August 25, 2021.  
- Declarations were signed by [[Abdel‑Karim Abulaban]] (Associate Civil Engineer) and [[Ryan T. Casebeer]] (Mechanical Engineer), both employed by the California Energy Commission.  
- Key dates: testimony filed 8/11/2021; MMRP submission deadline 8/25/2021; declarations dated 7/29/2021 and 8/3/2021.
